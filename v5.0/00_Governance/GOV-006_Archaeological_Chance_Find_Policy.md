## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the protocol for responding to unexpected discoveries of potential archaeological artifacts or sites of cultural significance during field operations.

Aeria Solutions Ltd recognizes that:
- Our work frequently occurs in areas where artifacts may be present
- Archaeological finds may hold significant cultural, historical, or spiritual value
- Proper handling of chance finds is both a legal obligation and an ethical responsibility
- Indigenous cultural heritage requires particular respect and protection

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All field personnel conducting operations at any site
- All projects regardless of client or location
- All potential archaeological or cultural discoveries including but not limited to:
  - Artifacts (tools, pottery, weapons, ornaments)
  - Human remains
  - Culturally modified trees
  - Rock art or petroglyphs
  - Historical structures or foundations
  - Any item or feature that appears to be of cultural or historical significance

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 General Principle

If you see something that may be an artifact or item of cultural significance, **report it immediately**. When in doubt, treat it as significant.

### 5.2 Chance Find Protocol

Upon discovering a potential artifact or site of cultural significance:

**1. STOP**
- Immediately cease all work in the vicinity of the find
- Do not move, collect, or further disturb the item or area
- Establish a mental note of the exact location

**2. PROTECT**
- Mark or flag the area if possible without disturbing the find
- Keep personnel away from the immediate area
- Do not attempt to clean, excavate, or examine the find

**3. DOCUMENT**
- Note the GPS coordinates or location reference
- Take photographs from a distance if possible
- Record time and date of discovery
- Note what activities were occurring when the find was made

**4. REPORT**
- Immediately notify the Site Supervisor or Client Manager
- Report to any additional parties as directed by the Site Supervisor during pre-work briefings
- Do not discuss the find publicly or on social media

**5. WAIT**
- Do not resume work in the area until authorized by the Site Supervisor
- Follow all instructions from heritage professionals if they attend

### 5.3 Pre-Work Briefing

Before commencing work at any site:
- Confirm the reporting chain with the Site Supervisor or Client Manager
- Ask if there are any known archaeological sensitivities in the work area
- Clarify any site-specific protocols that may apply

### 5.4 Human Remains

If human remains are discovered:
- Follow the same Stop-Protect-Document-Report protocol
- Treat the area with the utmost respect and dignity
- This is a legal matter requiring immediate notification to authorities
- Do not disturb, photograph close-up, or discuss publicly

### 5.5 Confidentiality

- Location details of archaeological or cultural finds shall not be disclosed publicly
- Information shall only be shared with authorized personnel
- Social media posts about finds are prohibited

| Role | Responsibilities |

## 7. Training

All field personnel shall be briefed on this policy:
- During initial onboarding
- As part of pre-project briefings when working in archaeologically sensitive areas
- Annually as a refresher
