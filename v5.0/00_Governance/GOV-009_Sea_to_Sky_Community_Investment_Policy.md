## 1. Purpose

This policy formalizes Aeria Solutions Ltd's commitment to giving back to the Sea to Sky community that supports our business. We believe that local success should translate to local benefit.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All projects conducted within the Sea to Sky geographic area
- Revenue and profit calculations for qualifying projects
- Donation administration and tracking

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Sea to Sky Region** | The geographic area from Vancouver to Lillooet, including the Sea to Sky Highway corridor and surrounding communities |
| **Qualifying Project** | Any project where the primary work site is located within the Sea to Sky Region |
| **Net Profit** | Project revenue minus direct costs (labour, equipment, travel, materials, subcontractors) |

---

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Commitment

For every qualifying project conducted within the Sea to Sky region, Aeria Solutions Ltd will donate to the **Squamish Search and Rescue Drone Program**:

| Calculation | Amount |
|:------------|:-------|
| **2% of Net Profit** | OR |
| **$500** | Whichever is **greater** |

### 4.2 Recipient Organization

**Squamish Search and Rescue — Drone Program**

Squamish Search and Rescue (Squamish SAR) is a volunteer organization providing emergency search and rescue services to the Sea to Sky corridor. Their drone program enhances search capabilities, particularly in challenging terrain and conditions.

We specifically support the drone program because:
- It directly aligns with our expertise and industry
- It provides tangible benefit to community safety
- It supports volunteer SAR members with modern technology
- It enhances search effectiveness in our local mountains

### 4.3 Conflict of Interest Disclosure

**Transparency Notice:**

Dustin Wales, CEO of Aeria Solutions Ltd, serves as the Drone Program Coordinator for Squamish Search and Rescue. This policy is disclosed with full transparency regarding this relationship.

**Safeguards:**
- Donation amounts are calculated per this policy, not at the discretion of the CEO
- Financial records of donations are maintained and available for review
- Squamish SAR provides receipts for all donations received
- This disclosure is included in the policy for transparency

We believe this relationship strengthens rather than compromises this commitment—the CEO's direct involvement in SAR ensures donations are used effectively for the drone program.

### 4.4 Calculation and Tracking

**For each qualifying project:**

1. Project location is confirmed as within the Sea to Sky region
2. Upon project completion, net profit is calculated
3. 2% of net profit is calculated
4. The greater of 2% or $500 is determined
5. Donation is processed to Squamish SAR Drone Program
6. Receipt is obtained and filed

**Example Calculations:**

| Project Net Profit | 2% Calculation | Minimum | Donation Amount |
|:-------------------|:---------------|:--------|:----------------|
| $5,000 | $100 | $500 | **$500** (minimum applies) |
| $30,000 | $600 | $500 | **$600** (2% applies) |
| $100,000 | $2,000 | $500 | **$2,000** (2% applies) |

### 4.5 Geographic Boundaries

**Included Communities:**
- Vancouver (City of Vancouver boundaries)
- North Vancouver / West Vancouver
- Lions Bay
- Squamish
- Whistler
- Pemberton
- Lillooet
- Communities along Highway 99 between Vancouver and Lillooet

**Determining Project Location:**
- The primary work site determines qualification
- If a project spans multiple locations, the majority work site applies
- If unclear, the project qualifies (we err on the side of giving)

### 4.6 Timing

- Donations are processed quarterly
- All qualifying projects completed in the quarter are included
- Annual summary is prepared for records
