## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Aeria Solutions Ltd's commitment to meaningful Indigenous participation, respectful engagement, and reconciliation in all aspects of our operations.

We recognize that:
- We operate on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories of Indigenous Peoples
- Indigenous communities have inherent rights to self-determination and governance
- Reconciliation requires action, not just acknowledgment
- Building genuine relationships takes time, respect, and good faith

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All operations conducted on Indigenous traditional territories
- All projects where Indigenous communities are clients or stakeholders
- All engagements with Indigenous individuals, communities, and organizations
- All personnel representing Aeria Solutions Ltd

---

## 3. Guiding Principles

### 3.1 Truth and Reconciliation

We are guided by the spirit and intent of the Truth and Reconciliation Commission Calls to Action, committing to:
- Acknowledge the historical and ongoing impacts of colonization
- Act with humility and openness to learning
- Support Indigenous-led initiatives where we can contribute meaningfully
- Conduct our business in ways that advance, not hinder, reconciliation

### 3.2 Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC)

We respect the right of Indigenous Peoples to Free, Prior and Informed Consent regarding activities that may affect their lands, territories, or resources. We commit to:
- Engaging early and transparently
- Providing complete information in accessible formats
- Respecting decisions made by Indigenous communities
- Never proceeding where genuine consent has not been given

### 3.3 Kindness, Courtesy, and Thoughtfulness

Our baseline approach to all Indigenous engagement is grounded in:
- Kindness in our interactions
- Courtesy in our communications
- Thoughtfulness in our actions
- Humility in our approach
- Patience in building relationships

---

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Engagement Protocol

**Before Project Commencement:**
- Identify whose traditional territory the project is located on
- Understand any territory-specific requirements or protocols
- Where appropriate, notify or engage with relevant Indigenous communities
- Follow client guidance on Indigenous engagement requirements

**During Operations:**
- Conduct ourselves as respectful guests on traditional territories
- Follow all territory-specific protocols communicated to us
- Be responsive to concerns raised by Indigenous community members
- Report any issues or sensitivities to the appropriate parties

### 4.2 Data Sovereignty

We recognize Indigenous data sovereignty and commit to the following:

| Principle | Our Commitment |
|:----------|:---------------|
| **Ownership** | Data collected on behalf of Indigenous clients belongs to them |
| **Control** | Indigenous clients control how their data is used, shared, and stored |
| **Access** | Indigenous clients have full access to all data within the scope of collection |
| **Possession** | We do not retain copies of Indigenous client data beyond project requirements without explicit permission |

**Specifically:**
- All data collected within the scope requested by an Indigenous client is their property
- We will not use, share, or publish Indigenous client data without explicit consent
- Data will be transferred and/or deleted according to client direction upon project completion
- We will implement appropriate security measures to protect Indigenous data during our possession

### 4.3 Capacity Building and Training

We are committed to building Indigenous capacity in RPAS operations through:

**Training Opportunities:**
- Offering training placements to Indigenous individuals where opportunities arise
- Providing pathways to Transport Canada RPAS certification (Basic and Advanced)
- Offering exposure and familiarization for those exploring the field
- Flexible scheduling to accommodate community and cultural commitments

**On-the-Job Learning:**
- Including Indigenous participants in operational tasks where available and appropriate
- Mentorship from experienced pilots
- Practical, hands-on experience alongside formal training

**Employment Pathways:**
- When qualified Indigenous individuals seek employment, we will give fair consideration
- We will share job opportunities with Indigenous partners and training participants
- We will support Indigenous entrepreneurs entering the RPAS industry where we can

### 4.4 Territory-Specific Requirements

We acknowledge that different Nations have different protocols, requirements, and expectations. We commit to:
- Learning and following territory-specific requirements for each project
- Asking questions respectfully when we are unsure
- Adapting our approach based on community guidance
- Never assuming one Nation's protocols apply to another

### 4.5 Working with Indigenous Clients

When Indigenous communities or organizations are our clients:
- We work collaboratively, not prescriptively
- We respect Indigenous decision-making processes and timelines
- We provide transparent pricing and clear deliverables
- We ensure all data sovereignty principles are upheld

---

## 5. What This Policy Is Not

This policy represents our genuine commitment to respectful engagement and reconciliation. It is:
- Not a marketing exercise
- Not a claim of Indigenous partnership where formal agreements do not exist
- Not a substitute for formal consultation processes where those are required
- Not a claim that we have all the answers—we are learning
