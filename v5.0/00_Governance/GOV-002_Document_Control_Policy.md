## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for creating, approving, distributing, revising, and retaining documents within the Aeria Solutions Ltd safety and operations program.

Effective document control ensures:
- Personnel have access to current, approved documents
- Outdated documents are removed from use
- Changes are tracked and authorized
- Regulatory and audit requirements are met

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All policies, procedures, manuals, and guides
- All forms, checklists, and templates
- All Formal Hazard Assessments (FHAs)
- All Quick Reference Cards (QRCs)
- All regulatory submission templates

This policy applies to all personnel who create, approve, distribute, or use controlled documents.

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 General Requirements

1. All controlled documents shall be:
   - Uniquely identified with a document number
   - Version controlled
   - Dated with effective date and review date
   - Approved before release
   - Accessible to personnel who need them

2. Only current, approved versions shall be used for operations.

3. Obsolete documents shall be clearly marked or removed from circulation.

### 5.2 Document Creation

1. New documents shall follow the standard format defined in GOV-002-PR.

2. New documents require approval by the appropriate authority before release:
   - Policies: Accountable Executive
   - Procedures: Operations Manager or document owner
   - Forms/Templates: Operations Manager

3. All documents shall include:
   - Document number and title
   - Version number
   - Effective date
   - Review date (typically 12 months from effective date)
   - Amendment history
   - Approval signature or indication

### 5.3 Document Review and Revision

1. All documents shall be reviewed at least annually or when:
   - Regulations change
   - Operations change significantly
   - Incidents reveal deficiencies
   - Audits identify gaps
   - Personnel identify improvement opportunities

2. Revisions shall be:
   - Documented in the amendment history
   - Approved before release
   - Distributed to replace previous versions
   - Communicated to affected personnel

3. Minor corrections (typos, formatting) may be made without full review but shall be noted in amendment history.

### 5.4 Document Distribution

1. Current versions shall be available:
   - Digitally via the document management system
   - In printed form where required for field use

2. Personnel shall be notified when documents affecting their duties are revised.

3. Printed copies are uncontrolled unless specifically issued as controlled copies.

### 5.5 Document Retention

1. Documents shall be retained per the following minimums:

| Document Type | Retention Period |
|:--------------|:-----------------|
| Current policies/procedures | Indefinite (while current) |
| Superseded versions | 3 years after superseded |
| Training records | Duration of employment + 2 years |
| Flight records | 2 years minimum |
| Maintenance records | Life of aircraft + 2 years |
| Incident/investigation records | 5 years |
| Audit records | 3 years |

2. Records shall be stored securely with appropriate backup.

### 5.6 Document Numbering

1. All documents shall use the numbering system defined in GOV-001 Master Program Index.

2. Document numbers shall not be reused for different documents.

3. Procedures related to a policy use the policy number with "-PR" suffix.

## 6. Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|:-----|:-----------------|
| **Accountable Executive** | Approve policies; ensure document control system is maintained; approve major revisions |
| **Operations Manager** | Approve procedures and forms; ensure documents are current; manage distribution; conduct reviews |
| **Document Owners** | Maintain assigned documents; initiate reviews; propose revisions |
| **All Personnel** | Use current versions only; report errors or needed updates; return obsolete copies |
