## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Aeria Solutions Ltd's commitment to accessibility, disability inclusion, and creating opportunities for people with disabilities in the RPAS industry.

We believe that:
- Disability should not be a barrier to participation in emerging technology fields
- Our industry benefits from diverse perspectives and abilities
- Inclusion requires intentional action, not just good intentions
- We have both a social responsibility and a business opportunity to be inclusive

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- Training programs offered by Aeria Solutions Ltd
- Employment and contracting opportunities
- Community partnerships and giving initiatives
- Workplace accommodations for personnel with disabilities

---

## 3. Policy

### 3.1 Community Partnership

We partner with local accessibility organizations to give back to our community and learn how we can be more inclusive.

**Current Partnership:**
- **Squamish Adaptive Mountain Biking** — We support adaptive recreation in our community and learn from their expertise in creating accessible experiences

We actively seek opportunities to:
- Support adaptive and accessible recreation and education programs
- Learn from disability advocacy organizations
- Apply accessibility learnings to our own programs

### 3.2 Training Accessibility

We are committed to making RPAS training accessible to people with disabilities:

**Cost Accommodation:**
- We offer significantly discounted training rates for individuals with disabilities
- Discount levels are determined case-by-case based on individual circumstances
- Financial barriers should not prevent capable individuals from entering this field

**Schedule Flexibility:**
- We provide flexible scheduling to accommodate medical appointments, therapy, and energy management
- Training can be spread over longer periods if needed
- We work around individual needs rather than rigid schedules

**Accessibility Accommodations:**
- We assess and arrange accessibility needs on a case-by-case basis
- We partner with local accessibility groups to understand specific requirements
- We are open to creative solutions to make training work

**What We Can Accommodate:**
- Modified training schedules and pacing
- Alternative assessment formats where regulations permit
- Physical accessibility arrangements for training locations
- Communication accommodations (written materials, visual aids, etc.)
- Breaks and rest periods as needed

### 3.3 Employment Pathways

We are committed to helping trained individuals with disabilities find work:

- When we train individuals with disabilities, we actively seek to connect them with employment opportunities
- We give fair consideration to qualified candidates with disabilities for our own positions
- We share job opportunities in the RPAS industry with training participants
- We provide references and support for individuals seeking work elsewhere

### 3.4 Workplace Inclusion

For personnel with disabilities working with Aeria Solutions Ltd:

- We provide reasonable accommodations to enable full participation
- We engage in open dialogue about what accommodations are needed
- We respect privacy regarding disability-related information
- We create an environment where people feel comfortable disclosing needs

### 3.5 Continuous Learning

We acknowledge we don't have all the answers. We commit to:

- Learning from disability advocacy organizations
- Listening to feedback from individuals with disabilities
- Updating our practices as we learn
- Being honest about our current limitations while working to improve

---

## 4. What We Cannot Accommodate

We are transparent about current limitations:

- Some field operations have inherent physical requirements that may not be modifiable
- Transport Canada regulations set certain requirements for pilot certification that we cannot waive
- Remote field sites may have accessibility barriers beyond our control

When we cannot accommodate, we will:
- Be honest and clear about why
- Explore alternatives where possible
- Provide referrals to other opportunities if we can

| Role | Responsibilities |
|:-----|:-----------------|
| **Accountable Executive** | Champion inclusion initiatives; maintain community partnerships; ensure policy compliance |
| **Operations Manager** | Coordinate training accommodations; assess accessibility needs; connect participants with opportunities |
| **All Personnel** | Create welcoming environments; respect accommodations; report accessibility barriers |

---

## 6. Requesting Accommodations

Individuals seeking accommodations should:

1. Contact us to discuss their interest in training or employment
2. Share (at their comfort level) what accommodations would be helpful
3. Work with us to develop an accommodation plan
4. Provide feedback on what is and isn't working

We will:
1. Listen without judgment
2. Consult with accessibility partners if needed (with permission)
3. Develop a practical accommodation plan
4. Follow through on our commitments
