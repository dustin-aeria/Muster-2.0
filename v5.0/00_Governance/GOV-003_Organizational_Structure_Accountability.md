## 1. Purpose

This document defines the organizational structure of Aeria Solutions Ltd and establishes clear accountability for safety, operations, and regulatory compliance. It fulfills requirements under:

- CAR 901.214 — RPOC organizational requirements
- CAR 901.215 — Operations Manager designation
- CAR 901.216 — Person Responsible for Maintenance designation
- BC OHS Regulation Part 3 — Employer responsibilities
- Energy Safety Canada SECOR Element A — Management commitment

## 2. Scope

This document applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions Ltd personnel
- All subcontractors working under Aeria direction
- All operations conducted under the Aeria RPOC

## 6. Operational Roles

### 6.1 Pilot in Command (PIC)

**Accountability:**

| Area | Responsibility |
|:-----|:---------------|
| **Flight Safety** | Responsible for safety of each flight conducted |
| **Go/No-Go** | Final authority on whether to commence or continue flight |
| **Risk Assessment** | Conducts pre-flight risk assessment and FLHA |
| **Emergency Authority** | Authority to deviate from procedures for safety |
| **Crew Coordination** | Leads crew briefings; coordinates crew activities |
| **Compliance** | Follows all applicable procedures and regulations |
| **Reporting** | Reports hazards, incidents, and defects |
| **Documentation** | Completes required flight documentation |

**Authority:**
- Refuse to fly if conditions are unsafe
- Abort flight at any time for safety
- Direct crew members during operations
- Deviate from procedures in emergencies

---

### 6.2 Visual Observer (VO)

**Accountability:**

| Area | Responsibility |
|:-----|:---------------|
| **Airspace Scan** | Continuous visual scan for traffic and hazards |
| **Hazard Alerting** | Immediately alert PIC to observed hazards |
| **Communication** | Clear, timely communication with PIC |
| **Crew Support** | Support PIC as directed |
| **Compliance** | Follow all applicable procedures |
| **Reporting** | Report safety concerns and observations |

**Authority:**
- Call for immediate avoidance action when hazard observed
- Request mission abort if safety concern identified

---

## 8. Communication and Reporting Lines

### 8.1 Normal Operations

```
Operational Personnel → Operations Manager → Accountable Executive
```

### 8.2 Safety Reporting

```
Any Personnel → Operations Manager (or directly to AE)
                    ↓
              Accountable Executive
```

*Note: Any person may report safety concerns directly to the AE at any time.*

### 8.3 Maintenance Issues

```
PIC (discovers defect) → PRM → (resolution) → PIC
```

### 8.4 Emergency Contact Chain

| Priority | Contact | When |
|:---------|:--------|:-----|
| 1 | Emergency Services (911) | Life-threatening emergencies |
| 2 | Operations Manager | All operational incidents |
| 3 | Accountable Executive | Serious incidents, regulatory issues |
| 4 | Transport Canada | Reportable occurrences per CAR 901.49 |

## 9. Succession and Absence

### 9.1 Accountable Executive Absence

| Duration | Coverage |
|:---------|:---------|
| Short-term (< 1 week) | Operations Manager has operational authority; major decisions deferred |
| Extended (> 1 week) | Formal delegation in writing to Operations Manager |
| Permanent | Board/ownership appoints new AE; notify Transport Canada |

### 9.2 Operations Manager Absence

| Duration | Coverage |
|:---------|:---------|
| Short-term | AE assumes operational oversight |
| Extended | AE or designated qualified person |

### 9.3 PRM Absence

| Duration | Coverage |
|:---------|:---------|
| Short-term | AE provides maintenance oversight |
| Extended | AE or qualified designee |

*Note: When one person holds multiple roles (as currently with Operations Manager and PRM), absence coverage defaults to AE.*

---

## 10. Organizational Changes

Any change to key personnel or organizational structure shall:

1. Be documented in this document
2. Be communicated to all personnel
3. Be reported to Transport Canada within 7 days (per CAR 901.214)
4. Include verification of qualifications for new personnel
5. Include appropriate handover of responsibilities
