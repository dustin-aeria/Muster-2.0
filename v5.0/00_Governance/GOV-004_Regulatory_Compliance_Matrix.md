## 1. Purpose

This matrix maps regulatory requirements to Aeria Solutions program documents, demonstrating how each requirement is addressed. Use this document to:

- Verify regulatory compliance during internal reviews
- Prepare for regulatory audits (Transport Canada, SECOR)
- Identify which documents address specific requirements
- Ensure no regulatory gaps exist

---

## 2. Scope

This matrix covers:
- Canadian Aviation Regulations Part IX (RPAS)
- Standard 922 (RPAS Safety Assurance)
- Transport Canada Advisory Circulars
- BC OHS Regulation / WorkSafeBC
- Energy Safety Canada SECOR (10 Elements)
