## 1. Purpose

This template provides guidance for preparing Special Flight Operations Certificate (SFOC) applications for cargo delivery operations using RPAS >25 kg MTOW, including Heavy RPAS (>150 kg MTOW) operations requiring direct Transport Canada engagement.

---

## 2. Regulatory Framework

### 2.1 SFOC Requirements by Aircraft Category

| Aircraft Category | MTOW | SFOC Required | TC Engagement |
|:------------------|:-----|:--------------|:--------------|
| Small RPAS | <25 kg | Situational | Standard |
| Medium RPAS | 25-150 kg | Yes | Standard |
| Heavy RPAS | >150 kg | Yes | **Direct engagement mandatory** |

### 2.2 Applicable Aircraft

| Aircraft | MTOW | Category |
|:---------|:-----|:---------|
| DJI FlyCart 30 | 95 kg | Medium RPAS |
| DJI FlyCart 100 | 220 kg | **Heavy RPAS** |
