## 1. Purpose

This plan establishes procedures for maintaining business operations during disruptive events and recovering from disasters. It ensures Aeria Solutions Ltd can continue serving clients and meeting regulatory obligations following significant disruptions.

---

## 2. Scope

This plan applies to:
- All business operations of Aeria Solutions Ltd
- All personnel, equipment, and facilities
- All critical business functions and systems
- Recovery from any event that disrupts normal operations

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd maintains the capability to respond to and recover from events that threaten business continuity. We prioritize safety, regulatory compliance, and client service while ensuring the company can survive and recover from significant disruptions.

## 5. Risk Scenarios

### 5.1 Equipment Loss

**Scenario:** Loss of RPAS equipment due to crash, theft, fire, or damage.

| Impact Level | Description |
|:-------------|:------------|
| Single aircraft | Continue operations with remaining fleet |
| Multiple aircraft | Reduced operational capacity, priority scheduling |
| Total fleet loss | Operations suspended pending replacement |

**Recovery Procedures:**
1. Assess extent of loss and document for insurance
2. Notify affected clients of potential delays
3. Prioritize operations based on contract obligations
4. Initiate equipment replacement through insurance
5. Consider rental/lease equipment for immediate needs
6. Document lessons learned and implement preventive measures

**Recovery Resources:**
- Insurance policy and claim procedures
- Equipment supplier contacts
- Rental/lease equipment sources
- Client communication templates

### 5.2 Key Person Loss

**Scenario:** Temporary or permanent loss of key personnel (illness, injury, resignation).

| Key Role | Impact | Coverage Strategy |
|:---------|:-------|:------------------|
| Accountable Executive | Cannot operate RPOC | Designated alternate, regulatory notification |
| Pilot-in-Command | Reduced flight capacity | Cross-trained personnel, contract pilots |
| Operations Manager | Coordination gaps | Documented procedures, backup assignments |
| Maintenance personnel | Maintenance delays | External AMO arrangements |

**Recovery Procedures:**
1. Assess duration and extent of absence
2. Activate backup coverage arrangements
3. Notify Transport Canada if affecting RPOC positions (within required timeframe)
4. Redistribute responsibilities per documented procedures
5. Arrange external resources if needed
6. Document temporary arrangements

**Key Person Coverage:**
- All critical roles have documented procedures
- Cross-training ensures multiple personnel can perform essential functions
- External contractor relationships maintained for surge capacity
- Succession planning documented for RPOC positions

### 5.3 Natural Disaster

**Scenario:** Natural disasters affecting operations (earthquake, flood, wildfire, severe weather).

| Disaster Type | Primary Impact | Secondary Impact |
|:--------------|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Earthquake | Facility damage, road closures | Equipment damage, communication loss |
| Flood | Facility access, equipment damage | Client site access |
| Wildfire | Evacuation, smoke/visibility | Client projects affected |
| Severe weather | Operations suspended | Travel and access issues |

**Recovery Procedures:**
1. Ensure personnel safety (priority)
2. Assess facility and equipment damage
3. Establish communication with all personnel
4. Document damage for insurance
5. Notify clients of operational impacts
6. Implement remote operations if facility compromised
7. Coordinate with emergency services as required

**BC-Specific Considerations:**
- Earthquake preparedness (grab-and-go kits, structural assessment)
- Wildfire evacuation procedures (BC Wildfire Service alerts)
- Atmospheric river flooding response
- Winter storm power outage contingencies

### 5.4 Cyber Incident

**Scenario:** Cyber attack, ransomware, data breach, or system failure.

| Incident Type | Impact | Recovery Priority |
|:--------------|:-------|:------------------|
| Ransomware | System access denied | Restore from backup, isolate affected systems |
| Data breach | Client/company data exposed | Contain, assess, notify, remediate |
| System failure | Operations disrupted | Failover to backup systems |
| Website/email outage | Communication impaired | Alternative communication, service restoration |

**Recovery Procedures:**
1. Isolate affected systems immediately
2. Assess scope and nature of incident
3. Activate backup systems and data recovery
4. Engage cybersecurity support if needed
5. Notify affected parties per privacy requirements
6. Document incident and response
7. Implement additional security measures

### 5.5 Pandemic/Health Emergency

**Scenario:** Widespread illness affecting personnel availability or requiring operational changes.

**Recovery Procedures:**
1. Implement health and safety protocols
2. Assess personnel availability
3. Enable remote work where possible
4. Prioritize essential operations
5. Communicate with clients about capacity
6. Follow public health guidance
7. Document operational modifications

## 8. Backup and Data Recovery

### 8.1 Data Backup Strategy

| Data Category | Backup Method | Frequency | Retention |
|:--------------|:--------------|:----------|:----------|
| Flight records | Cloud backup, local copy | Daily | 10 years |
| Financial records | Cloud accounting, local backup | Daily | 7 years |
| Client data | Cloud storage, project backup | Per project | Per contract |
| Training records | Cloud backup | Weekly | Employment + 3 years |
| Safety records | Cloud backup, local copy | Daily | 10 years |
| System configurations | Cloud backup | Weekly | Current + 1 version |

### 8.2 Backup Locations

| Type | Primary | Secondary |
|:-----|:--------|:----------|
| Cloud storage | Supabase (Muster platform) | Additional cloud provider |
| Local backup | Office NAS/external drive | Off-site secure location |
| Paper records | Office filing | Scanned copies in cloud |

### 8.3 Recovery Procedures

**Data Recovery:**
1. Identify scope of data loss
2. Access most recent backup
3. Verify backup integrity
4. Restore to operational systems
5. Verify restored data accuracy
6. Document recovery process

**Recovery Time Objectives:**
| System | RTO |
|:-------|:----|
| Critical safety records | 4 hours |
| Financial systems | 24 hours |
| Client project data | 24 hours |
| Training records | 72 hours |
| Marketing/website | 1 week |

### 8.4 Testing

- Backup restoration tested quarterly
- Recovery procedures reviewed annually
- Test results documented and issues addressed
