## 1. Purpose

This guide establishes requirements and expectations for contractors and subcontractors performing work for or with Aeria Solutions Ltd. It ensures consistent standards for safety, quality, and professionalism across all operations.

---

## 2. Scope

This guide applies to:
- Independent contractors providing RPAS services
- Subcontractors working on Aeria projects
- Specialized service providers (survey, payload operators)
- Equipment or vehicle providers
- Any third party performing work under Aeria direction

---

## 3. Contractor Categories

| Category | Description | Examples |
|:---------|:------------|:---------|
| RPAS Operator | Licensed pilots providing flight services | Contract PICs, specialized pilots |
| Technical Specialist | Payload or data specialists | LiDAR operators, photogrammetrists |
| Ground Support | Non-flying support personnel | Observers, drivers, safety personnel |
| Equipment Provider | Equipment without operator | Vehicle rental, specialized gear |
| Professional Services | Technical or professional services | Surveyors, engineers, consultants |

## 4. Pre-Qualification Requirements

### 4.1 General Requirements (All Contractors)

| Requirement | Documentation |
|:------------|:--------------|
| Legal business status | Business registration or sole proprietor registration |
| WorkSafeBC compliance | Active account or letter of coverage |
| Insurance coverage | Certificate of Insurance meeting minimums |
| Banking information | For payment processing |
| Emergency contact | 24-hour contact information |

### 4.2 RPAS Operator Requirements

| Requirement | Documentation | Minimum Standard |
|:------------|:--------------|:-----------------|
| Pilot Certificate | Transport Canada certificate | Advanced Operations or higher |
| Medical declaration | Current medical | Per Transport Canada requirements |
| Knowledge test | RPAS knowledge certificate | Appropriate for operation type |
| Flight review | Current flight review | Within 24 months |
| Insurance | Aviation liability | $2,000,000 minimum |
| Own RPOC (if applicable) | RPOC certificate | If operating under own certificate |

### 4.3 Additional Requirements by Operation Type

| Operation Type | Additional Requirements |
|:---------------|:------------------------|
| Level 1 Complex (L1C) | L1C endorsement, appropriate insurance |
| BVLOS | BVLOS training, SFOC experience |
| Night operations | Night rating/endorsement |
| Specialized payload | Payload-specific certification |
| Industrial sites | Site-specific safety training |

### 4.4 Verification Process

1. Contractor submits pre-qualification package
2. Operations Manager reviews documentation
3. Verification of certificates with issuing authorities
4. Insurance coverage confirmed with broker
5. Contractor added to approved list
6. Re-verification annually or upon expiry

## 5. Safety Expectations

### 5.1 Safety Commitment

All contractors must:
- Prioritize safety above schedule or cost
- Stop work if unsafe conditions exist
- Report all hazards and incidents
- Comply with Aeria safety requirements
- Follow PIC instructions during operations

### 5.2 Regulatory Compliance

| Requirement | Expectation |
|:------------|:------------|
| Canadian Aviation Regulations | Full compliance at all times |
| Transport Canada directives | Immediate compliance |
| WorkSafeBC requirements | Full compliance |
| Site-specific regulations | Compliance with all applicable rules |

### 5.3 Pre-Flight Requirements

Before any flight operation, contractors must:
- Complete site risk assessment
- Brief all personnel on hazards
- Verify equipment airworthiness
- Confirm weather suitability
- Establish emergency procedures
- Communicate with Aeria PIC/Operations

### 5.4 During Operations

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| Crew Resource Management | Effective communication, shared situational awareness |
| Fitness for duty | No operations if fatigued, ill, or impaired |
| PPE compliance | Appropriate PPE worn at all times |
| Flight discipline | Operations within certificate limits |
| Documentation | Complete all required records |

### 5.5 Prohibited Conduct

The following result in immediate termination:
- Operating while impaired
- Falsifying records or certifications
- Reckless or negligent operation
- Violating aviation regulations
- Refusing to stop unsafe operations
- Harassment or unprofessional conduct

## 10. Incident Reporting

### 10.1 Reportable Incidents

All contractors must report:
- Any aviation incident or accident
- Injury to any person
- Property damage
- Near-miss events
- Equipment failures affecting safety
- Regulatory violations or concerns
- Security incidents

### 10.2 Reporting Procedure

**Immediate (within 1 hour):**
1. Ensure scene is safe
2. Provide first aid if needed
3. Call emergency services if required (911)
4. Notify Aeria PIC or Operations Manager
5. Preserve evidence and scene

**Same Day:**
1. Complete written incident report
2. Submit to Operations Manager
3. Preserve all relevant records
4. Document witness information

**Follow-Up:**
1. Cooperate with investigation
2. Provide additional information as requested
3. Participate in lessons learned review

### 10.3 Reportable Occurrences (Transport Canada)

Contractors must assist Aeria in reporting:
- Accidents (injury, property damage)
- Incidents per CAR 901.63
- Occurrences that must be reported immediately
- Occurrences to be reported within specific timeframes
