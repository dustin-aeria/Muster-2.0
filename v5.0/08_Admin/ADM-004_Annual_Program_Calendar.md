## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the annual calendar for safety, operational, and administrative program activities. It ensures consistent scheduling and completion of recurring tasks required for regulatory compliance, safety management, and operational excellence.

---

## 2. Scope

This calendar applies to:
- All safety management system activities
- Regulatory compliance deadlines
- Training and competency requirements
- Equipment maintenance and audits
- Management review and continuous improvement

## 6. Regulatory Deadlines

### 6.1 RPOC Requirements

| Requirement | Frequency | Renewal Period | Lead Time |
|:------------|:----------|:---------------|:----------|
| RPOC Certificate | Annual | 12 months | 60 days before expiry |
| Pilot Certificate | As per certificate | Per TC requirements | 90 days before expiry |
| Medical declaration | Annual | 12 months | 30 days before expiry |
| Insurance certificate | Annual | 12 months | 60 days before expiry |

### 6.2 COR and SECOR Requirements

| Requirement | Frequency | Timing |
|:------------|:----------|:-------|
| SECOR audit | Annual | As scheduled by certifying partner |
| COR external audit | Every 3 years | Per certification cycle |
| Internal audit | Annual | Prior to external audit |
| Action plan follow-up | Ongoing | Per audit findings |

### 6.3 Reporting Requirements

| Report | Frequency | Due Date | Submit To |
|:-------|:----------|:---------|:----------|
| RPOC annual report | Annual | Per RPOC renewal | Transport Canada |
| Incident reports | As required | Within 24-72 hours | Transport Canada (if applicable) |
| Occurrence reports | As required | Per regulatory requirement | TSB (if applicable) |
| WCB reports | As required | Per incident | WorkSafeBC |

## 8. Calendar Management

### 8.1 Scheduling Tools

- Electronic calendar with automated reminders
- Shared team calendar for visibility
- 30-day advance notice for major activities
- 7-day advance notice for routine tasks

### 8.2 Tracking and Accountability

| Element | Method |
|:--------|:-------|
| Task assignment | Calendar invites to responsible parties |
| Completion tracking | Confirmation when tasks completed |
| Overdue items | Weekly review of outstanding items |
| Documentation | Records filed per retention policy |

### 8.3 Calendar Adjustments

- Operational demands may require rescheduling
- Safety-critical items take priority
- Regulatory deadlines are not flexible
- Document any schedule changes
