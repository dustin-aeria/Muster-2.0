## 1. Purpose

This document provides standardized safety briefing materials for clients prior to and during RPAS operations. It ensures clients understand operational hazards, their responsibilities, and emergency procedures.

---

## 2. Scope

This package applies to:
- All clients observing RPAS operations
- Client representatives on active work sites
- Third parties with legitimate access to flight operations
- Property owners and stakeholders near flight areas

---

## 3. Pre-Project Safety Information

### 3.1 Introduction to RPAS Operations

Before operations commence, clients receive information on:

| Topic | Description |
|:------|:------------|
| RPAS overview | Basic aircraft capabilities and limitations |
| Flight profiles | Expected flight patterns, altitudes, and durations |
| Noise levels | Typical sound characteristics of RPAS operations |
| Visual impact | What to expect visually during operations |
| Weather dependencies | Conditions that may delay or cancel operations |

### 3.2 Site Preparation Requirements

Clients must ensure:
- Access routes are clear and identified
- Designated observation areas are available
- Site personnel are informed of operations
- Ground hazards are communicated to Aeria personnel
- Animals are secured if applicable

### 3.3 Scheduling and Timing

| Item | Client Responsibility |
|:-----|:----------------------|
| Start times | Confirm 24 hours in advance |
| Weather delays | Allow flexibility for weather holds |
| Access windows | Ensure site access during operational periods |
| Personnel availability | Provide point of contact on-site |

## 4. Site-Specific Hazard Communication

### 4.1 Pre-Flight Hazard Briefing

Before each operation, the Pilot-in-Command (PIC) will brief clients on:

| Hazard Category | Examples |
|:----------------|:---------|
| Flight hazards | Propeller danger zones, emergency landing areas |
| Site hazards | Terrain, obstacles, wildlife, weather exposure |
| Equipment hazards | Batteries, charging equipment, hot surfaces |
| Environmental hazards | Sun exposure, wind chill, precipitation |

### 4.2 BC-Specific Hazards

Operating in British Columbia requires awareness of:

| Hazard | Communication |
|:-------|:--------------|
| Wildlife | Bear and cougar awareness, seasonal activity |
| Terrain | Steep slopes, loose rock, unstable ground |
| Weather | Rapid changes, fog, wind funneling |
| Remote locations | Limited cell service, evacuation challenges |
| Seasonal conditions | Winter cold exposure, summer wildfire smoke |

### 4.3 Restricted Areas

Clients will be informed of:
- No-go zones during flight operations
- Minimum safe distances from aircraft
- Areas reserved for emergency landing
- Equipment staging areas (no unauthorized access)

| WorkSafeBC | 1-888-621-7233 | Workplace incidents |

### 5.2 Emergency Response Procedures

**In case of RPAS emergency (crash, flyaway, loss of control):**
1. Move away from aircraft immediately
2. Do not approach downed aircraft
3. Follow PIC instructions
4. Account for all personnel
5. Report any injuries to PIC

**In case of medical emergency:**
1. Alert PIC immediately
2. Call 911 if life-threatening
3. Administer first aid if trained
4. Provide location details to emergency services
5. Clear access route for emergency vehicles

**In case of fire:**
1. Alert all personnel
2. Move upwind and away from fire
3. Call 911
4. Do not attempt to fight battery fires with water
5. Use ABC extinguisher if safe to do so

### 5.3 Evacuation Procedures

- Primary and alternate evacuation routes identified at site briefing
- Muster point designated before operations begin
- Head count conducted after any evacuation
- PIC maintains accountability for all personnel

## 7. PPE Requirements for Client Observers

### 7.1 Minimum PPE Requirements

All client observers on active sites must wear:

| PPE Item | Standard | Provided By |
|:---------|:---------|:------------|
| High-visibility vest | CSA Z96 Class 2 | Aeria (loaner available) |
| Safety footwear | CSA Grade 1 | Client |
| Eye protection | CSA Z94.3 (when required) | Client |
| Hard hat | CSA Z94.1 (when required) | Client |

### 7.2 Site-Specific PPE

Additional PPE may be required based on site conditions:

| Condition | Additional PPE |
|:----------|:---------------|
| Construction site | Hard hat, safety boots mandatory |
| Utility inspection | Arc flash rated clothing if near energized equipment |
| Mining/industrial | Hearing protection, respiratory protection |
| Remote wilderness | Bear spray, communication device |
| Marine operations | PFD, marine footwear |
| Winter operations | Cold weather gear, traction aids |

### 7.3 PPE Inspection

- Client PPE must be in serviceable condition
- Damaged PPE must be replaced before site access
- Aeria reserves right to deny access for inadequate PPE
