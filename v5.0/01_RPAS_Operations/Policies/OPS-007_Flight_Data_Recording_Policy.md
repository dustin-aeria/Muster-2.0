## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for recording, retaining, and managing flight data to support safe operations, regulatory compliance, and incident investigation.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All RPAS flight operations
- Flight logs, telemetry, and recorded data
- All personnel involved in flight operations

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Flight Logging Requirements

**Every flight shall be logged.**

The flight log shall include:
- Date
- PIC name
- Aircraft registration/identifier
- Location (coordinates or description)
- Flight start and end times
- Total flight time
- Type of operation (VLOS/BVLOS, category)
- Purpose of flight
- Any incidents or occurrences
- Weather conditions
- Crew members
- Payload (if any)

### 4.2 Flight Log Methods

| Method | When Used |
|:-------|:----------|
| **Paper Log** | Backup; field conditions |
| **Digital Log** | Primary method |
| **Automated Logging** | Aircraft with automatic recording |

All methods shall capture required information.

### 4.3 Telemetry Data

Where available, retain telemetry data including:
- Flight path (GPS track)
- Altitude data
- Battery telemetry
- System warnings/alerts
- Control inputs
- C2 link status

**Telemetry shall be downloaded after each flight day and backed up.**

### 4.4 Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|:----------|:-----------------|
| **Flight logs** | 10 years |
| **Telemetry (routine operations)** | 1 year |
| **Telemetry (incidents/occurrences)** | 10 years |
| **Pre-flight/post-flight checklists** | 3 years |
| **Maintenance records** | See MCM |
| **Training records** | See TCP |

### 4.5 Incident Data Preservation

**If any incident or occurrence:**
1. Do not delete or overwrite data
2. Immediately preserve all flight data
3. Download telemetry before data is overwritten
4. Store separately with incident reference
5. Make copies for investigation

### 4.6 Data Security

Flight data shall be:
- Stored securely
- Backed up regularly
- Protected from unauthorized access
- Available for regulatory inspection if required

### 4.7 Flight Time Tracking

**Track for each pilot:**
- Total flight time (rolling 12 months)
- Flight time by aircraft type
- BVLOS flight time (separately)
- Training flight time

**Track for each aircraft:**
- Total flight hours
- Flights since maintenance
- Battery cycles

### 4.8 Reporting

Monthly reporting includes:
- Total flights
- Total flight hours
- Incidents/occurrences
- Pilot currency status
- Aircraft utilization

Operations Manager reviews monthly reports.

### 4.9 Regulatory Access

Flight records shall be made available to Transport Canada upon request:
- Within 48 hours for serious investigations
- Within 30 days for routine requests
- In format readable by the authority
