## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for the safe conduct of Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (RPAS) flight operations under Aeria Solutions' RPAS Operator Certificate (RPOC).

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All RPAS flight operations conducted under Aeria Solutions RPOC (930355)
- All pilots, visual observers, and ground crew
- All aircraft operated under the certificate
- Flight school operations under L1C Declaration

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 General Requirements

1. All RPAS operations shall be conducted in compliance with:
   - Canadian Aviation Regulations Part IX
   - Transport Canada Standard 922
   - Aeria Solutions RPOC Operations Manual
   - Applicable SFOCs (when required)

2. Flight operations shall only be conducted by qualified and authorized personnel.

3. Safety of persons and property is the paramount consideration for all operations.

### 5.2 Operational Categories

Aeria Solutions conducts operations in the following categories:

| Category | Requirements | Reference |
|:---------|:-------------|:----------|
| **Basic Operations** | Pilot Certificate - Basic, registered aircraft | CAR 901.29-901.44 |
| **Advanced Operations** | Pilot Certificate - Advanced, registered aircraft, reviewed RPA | CAR 901.45-901.66 |
| **BVLOS (L1 Complex)** | Declaration accepted, approved procedures | CAR 901.67-901.76 |
| **SFOC Operations** | Valid SFOC, compliance with conditions | CAR 901.77-901.87 |
| **Flight Training** | L1C Declaration, Chief Flight Instructor approval | CAR 901.67-901.76 |

### 5.3 Flight Crew Qualifications

| Role | Minimum Requirements |
|:-----|:--------------------|
| **PIC (Basic)** | Pilot Certificate - Basic Operations |
| **PIC (Advanced)** | Pilot Certificate - Advanced Operations |
| **PIC (BVLOS)** | Advanced Certificate + BVLOS ground school + company authorization |
| **Visual Observer** | Training per OPS-008-PR; no certificate required |

All pilots shall:
- Hold valid pilot certificate
- Be current (recency per CAR 901.68)
- Be authorized on aircraft type
- Be familiar with applicable procedures

### 5.4 Aircraft Requirements

Aircraft shall:
- Be registered with Transport Canada (or exempt)
- Be on the Aeria Solutions aircraft registry
- Be maintained per Maintenance Control Manual
- Have valid airworthiness status
- Be configured per approved operations

### 5.5 Pre-Flight Requirements

Before any flight:
1. Site survey/assessment completed
2. NOTAM check completed
3. Airspace authorization obtained (if required)
4. Weather assessment completed
5. FLHA completed
6. Pre-flight inspection completed
7. Crew briefing completed
8. Emergency procedures reviewed

### 5.6 Flight Conduct

During flight operations:
- PIC maintains command authority
- Visual observers maintain required separation
- Operations remain within approved parameters
- Emergency procedures are immediately available
- Communication is maintained between crew
- Flight is terminated if safety is compromised

### 5.7 Post-Flight Requirements

After each flight:
1. Post-flight inspection completed
2. Flight log completed
3. Incidents/occurrences reported
4. Equipment properly stored
5. Batteries managed per MCM procedures

### 5.8 Restricted Operations

The following require additional authorization:

| Operation | Authorization Required |
|:----------|:----------------------|
| BVLOS | L1C Declaration + PIC authorization |
| Night operations | SFOC |
| Over people not involved | Advanced + conditions met |
| Controlled airspace | NAV CANADA authorization |
| Restricted areas | Appropriate authority permission |

### 5.9 Prohibited Operations

The following are prohibited:
- Flight while impaired
- Flight without required authorizations
- Flight above 400 ft AGL without authorization
- Flight near emergency scenes
- Flight in a reckless manner
- Flight contrary to regulations or company procedures
