## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for conducting RPAS operations at client sites, including industrial facilities, construction sites, and other controlled locations.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All operations at client-controlled sites
- Industrial, construction, and other commercial sites
- Personnel working under client direction or supervision

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 General Requirements

When operating at client sites:
1. Comply with all Aeria policies and procedures
2. Comply with client safety requirements
3. Where conflicts exist, follow the more restrictive requirement
4. Maintain open communication with client representatives

### 4.2 Pre-Work Requirements

Before mobilizing to a client site:

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Client Contact** | Identify client representative and contact |
| **Site Access** | Obtain access permissions/badging |
| **Orientation** | Complete required client orientations |
| **Documentation** | Provide certificates, insurance as required |
| **Permits** | Obtain required work permits |
| **Communication** | Establish radio channels if required |

### 4.3 Client Orientation

Complete client-required orientations including:
- Site-specific hazards
- Emergency procedures
- Muster points
- Restricted areas
- Communication protocols
- PPE requirements

Document completion and retain records.

### 4.4 Work Permits

Many industrial sites require work permits for RPAS:

| Permit Type | Common Requirements |
|:------------|:-------------------|
| **Hot Work** | If near flammable materials |
| **Working at Heights** | Some sites classify RPAS |
| **Aviation Permit** | Site-specific aviation control |
| **General Work Permit** | Standard authorization |

**Do not operate without required permits.**

### 4.5 Site-Specific Safety

Beyond Aeria requirements, comply with:
- Client PPE requirements (may exceed ours)
- Site speed limits
- Designated walkways and travel routes
- No-fly zones within site
- Restricted photography areas
- Drug and alcohol policies

### 4.6 Emergency Procedures

Know and follow:
- Site emergency signals/alarms
- Evacuation routes and muster points
- Emergency contact numbers
- First aid locations
- Site-specific hazards (H2S, confined spaces, etc.)

### 4.7 Coordination

**Before flying:**
- Notify client representative
- Coordinate with other activities
- Identify any conflicts (cranes, radio frequencies, etc.)
- Confirm no-fly zones
- Establish communication protocol

**During operations:**
- Stay in contact with client representative
- Report any incidents immediately
- Coordinate changes to plan
- Monitor radio channels as required

### 4.8 Security and Confidentiality

Many client sites have confidentiality requirements:
- Do not photograph restricted areas
- Handle imagery per client requirements
- Do not discuss client operations externally
- Follow data handling instructions
- Sign NDAs as required

### 4.9 Incident Reporting

If incident occurs at client site:
1. Follow emergency procedures
2. Notify client representative immediately
3. Follow Aeria incident reporting procedures
4. Complete client incident reports as required
5. Cooperate with client investigation

### 4.10 Site-Specific Documentation

Maintain for each client site:
- Site safety information
- Client contacts
- Orientation records
- Permits and authorizations
- Previous flight logs
- Lessons learned

## 5. Industrial Site Considerations

### Oil & Gas Sites
- H2S awareness and monitoring
- Explosion-proof/intrinsically safe requirements
- Flare exclusion zones
- Process safety considerations

### Construction Sites
- Crane coordination
- Changing conditions
- Multiple contractors
- Elevated work coordination

### Mining Sites
- Blasting schedules
- Mobile equipment
- Communication zones
- Dust considerations

### Power/Utility Sites
- EMI considerations
- High voltage exclusion
- Coordination with operations
