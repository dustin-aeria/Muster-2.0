## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for obtaining airspace authorizations and operating within Canadian airspace in compliance with CARs Part IX.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All RPAS operations conducted under Aeria RPOC
- All airspace classes and types
- All personnel involved in flight planning

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Airspace Classification

| Class | Description | RPAS Authorization |
|:------|:------------|:-------------------|
| **A** | High-level controlled | Not permitted (RPAS) |
| **B** | Major airport terminal | Authorization required |
| **C** | Medium airport terminal | Authorization required |
| **D** | Smaller tower-controlled | Authorization required |
| **E** | Controlled (no tower) | Varies; check requirements |
| **F** | Special use (restricted/advisory) | Depends on type |
| **G** | Uncontrolled | Standard RPAS rules apply |

### 4.2 Controlled Airspace Operations

**No RPAS operations in controlled airspace without prior authorization.**

Authorization methods:
- NAV CANADA RPAS Flight Authorization System (online)
- Direct coordination with ATC (for complex operations)
- SFOC (if required for specific operation)

### 4.3 Authorization Process

#### NAV CANADA Online System

1. Register account at NAV CANADA RPAS portal
2. Submit flight request minimum 24-48 hours in advance
3. Provide:
   - Operation location (coordinates/area)
   - Date and time window
   - Maximum altitude
   - RPOC number
   - PIC contact information
4. Await authorization
5. Do not operate until authorization received

#### Extended/Complex Operations

For operations requiring ongoing or complex coordination:
- Contact Operations Manager to coordinate
- May require direct ATC liaison
- Allow additional lead time

### 4.4 Flight Planning Requirements

Before any flight, verify:
1. **Airspace Type** — Check charts, apps, or NavCanada resources
2. **Authorization Required?** — Determine if authorization needed
3. **Obtain Authorization** — Before proceeding to site
4. **NOTAM Check** — Review NOTAMs affecting area
5. **Temporary Flight Restrictions** — Check for TFRs

### 4.5 Altitude Restrictions

| Operation Type | Maximum Altitude |
|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Basic Operations | 400 ft AGL |
| Advanced Operations | 400 ft AGL (unless authorized) |
| Near buildings/structures | May exceed 400 ft per CAR 901.19 |
| Controlled airspace | As authorized |

### 4.6 Distance Requirements

| Feature | Minimum Distance |
|:--------|:-----------------|
| Aerodromes (uncontrolled) | 3 NM unless authorized |
| Aerodromes (controlled) | As per authorization |
| Heliports | Per regulation |
| Built-up areas | Per operation category |
| Emergency scenes | Maintain safe distance; 5 NM minimum |

### 4.7 Transponder and ADS-B Requirements

Certain controlled airspace operations may require transponder or ADS-B equipment.

| Situation | Requirement |
|:----------|:------------|
| **Standard RPAS operations** | Transponder not typically required |
| **ATC coordination operations** | ATC may require transponder for traffic separation |
| **Specific SFOC conditions** | May require transponder/ADS-B as condition |
| **High-traffic controlled airspace** | Verify requirements with NAV CANADA |

**If transponder/ADS-B required:**
- Verify equipment compatibility with RPA
- Confirm ATC can see transponder returns
- Follow specific ATC instructions
- Document equipment serial and mode in flight plan

**Note:** Most small RPA cannot carry traditional transponders. If ATC requires transponder for the operation, alternative arrangements (reduced operation hours, different location, etc.) may be necessary. Coordinate with Operations Manager before accepting transponder-required operations.

### 4.8 Special Use Airspace

**Restricted Areas:**
- Require permission from controlling authority
- Check CFS/AIP for contact information
- Obtain written authorization

**Advisory Areas:**
- Heightened vigilance required
- Follow any applicable procedures
- Check NOTAMs

**Military Operations Areas:**
- Coordinate with controlling authority
- Be aware of military traffic

### 4.9 NOTAM Requirements

Check NOTAMs for:
- Temporary restricted airspace
- Special events
- Military exercises
- Forest fire areas (flight restricted)
- Other relevant information

Check NOTAMs:
- Before flight planning
- Again before departure to site
- Using NavCanada briefing or approved apps

### 4.10 Documentation

Retain documentation of:
- Airspace authorization requests and approvals
- NOTAM checks
- Flight plan details
- Any communications with ATC/authorities
