## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes qualification requirements for personnel conducting RPAS operations under Aeria Solutions RPOC.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All pilots operating under Aeria RPOC
- Visual observers
- Ground crew and support personnel
- Flight training staff

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Pilot Certificate Requirements

#### Basic Operations Pilot

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Certificate** | Pilot Certificate - Basic Operations |
| **Age** | Minimum 14 years |
| **Knowledge** | Pass Small Basic Examination |
| **Recency** | Review within 24 months |

#### Advanced Operations Pilot

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Certificate** | Pilot Certificate - Advanced Operations |
| **Age** | Minimum 16 years |
| **Knowledge** | Pass Small Advanced Examination |
| **Flight Review** | Advanced flight review |
| **Recency** | Review within 24 months |

### 4.2 Company Authorization Requirements

Beyond regulatory requirements, Aeria pilots shall:

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Company Training** | Complete Aeria orientation and procedures training |
| **Aircraft Type** | Complete type-specific training and checkout |
| **SOPs** | Demonstrate knowledge of company SOPs |
| **SMS** | Complete SMS awareness training |
| **Currency** | Meet company currency requirements |
| **Medical** | Self-declare fitness per CAR 901.68 |

### 4.3 BVLOS Pilot Requirements

In addition to Advanced requirements:

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Ground School** | Complete BVLOS ground training |
| **Practical Training** | Supervised BVLOS flights |
| **Assessment** | Pass BVLOS competency check |
| **Operations Manager Approval** | Written authorization for BVLOS |
| **Recency** | BVLOS flight within 90 days |

### 4.4 Visual Observer Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Training** | Complete VO training per OPS-008-PR |
| **Briefing** | Pre-flight briefing before each operation |
| **Vision** | Sufficient to maintain visual contact with aircraft |
| **Communication** | Ability to communicate with PIC |

Visual observers do not require a pilot certificate.

### 4.5 Ground Crew Requirements

Ground crew supporting operations shall:
- Complete safety orientation
- Understand their role in operations
- Be briefed on hazards and emergency procedures
- Follow PIC direction

### 4.6 Flight Instructor Requirements

Flight instructors under L1C Declaration shall:
- Hold Pilot Certificate - Advanced Operations
- Complete instructor training program
- Be approved by Chief Flight Instructor
- Maintain currency per flight school requirements

Chief Flight Instructor: Dustin Wales

### 4.7 Currency Requirements

| Role | Currency Requirement |
|:-----|:--------------------|
| **PIC (all operations)** | 1 flight per 90 days on aircraft type |
| **PIC (BVLOS)** | 1 BVLOS flight per 90 days |
| **PIC (complex operations)** | As specified in operation manual |
| **Instructor** | Per flight school requirements |

If currency lapses:
- Refresher training required before resuming operations
- May require checkout with qualified pilot

### 4.8 Records

The following records shall be maintained:

| Record | Retention |
|:-------|:----------|
| Pilot certificates (copies) | Current + 2 years |
| Company training records | Duration of employment + 3 years |
| Flight reviews | 5 years |
| Type authorizations | Current |
| Currency tracking | Rolling 12 months |

### 4.9 Fitness for Duty

Pilots shall not fly if:
- Impaired by fatigue, illness, medication, or substances
- Not confident in ability to safely conduct flight
- Medical condition affects ability to perform duties

See HSE-013 Fit for Duty Policy.

## 5. Authorization Process

### New Pilot Onboarding

1. Verify valid pilot certificate
2. Complete company orientation training
3. Complete SMS training
4. Complete aircraft type training
5. Demonstrate competency (check ride)
6. Issue company authorization
7. Enter in pilot roster

### Adding Aircraft Type

1. Complete aircraft-specific ground training
2. Complete practical training on type
3. Pass competency check
4. Update pilot authorization

### BVLOS Authorization

1. Complete ground training
2. Complete practical training (supervised flights)
3. Pass BVLOS assessment
4. Operations Manager reviews
5. Issue BVLOS authorization letter
