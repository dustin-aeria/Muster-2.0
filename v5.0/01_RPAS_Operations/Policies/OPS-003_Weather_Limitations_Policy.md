## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes weather limitations for RPAS operations to ensure safe flight conduct and compliance with regulatory requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All RPAS flight operations
- All aircraft types in the Aeria fleet
- All operating conditions

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 General Weather Requirements

**All RPAS operations shall only be conducted in conditions that:**
1. Meet regulatory minimums
2. Do not exceed aircraft limitations
3. Allow safe control of the aircraft
4. Allow visual observer duties to be performed
5. Are appropriate for the mission complexity

### 4.2 Regulatory Weather Minimums

| Condition | VLOS Requirement |
|:----------|:-----------------|
| **Visibility** | Sufficient to maintain VLOS at all times |
| **Cloud** | Clear of cloud |
| **Ceiling** | N/A (stay below cloud) |
| **Daylight** | Day VFR conditions (unless night SFOC) |

### 4.3 Company Weather Limitations

**Standard Operations:**

| Parameter | Limitation | Notes |
|:----------|:-----------|:------|
| **Wind (sustained)** | ≤25 km/h (15 mph) | Reduce for small aircraft |
| **Wind (gusts)** | ≤35 km/h (22 mph) | Reduce for small aircraft |
| **Visibility** | ≥3 km | Minimum for safe VLOS |
| **Precipitation** | None (light only for rated aircraft) | No flight in rain/snow unless approved |
| **Temperature** | Per aircraft limits | Typically 0°C to 40°C |
| **Icing conditions** | Prohibited | No flight in icing conditions |
| **Thunderstorms** | 30 km minimum distance | Cease operations if approaching |

**Aircraft-Specific Limits:**

Operations Manager maintains aircraft-specific weather limits based on:
- Manufacturer specifications
- Aircraft capability
- Operational experience

See individual aircraft flight manuals for specific limits.

### 4.4 BVLOS Weather Requirements

BVLOS operations have enhanced weather requirements:
- Visibility at all points along flight path
- No precipitation unless aircraft rated
- Wind within aircraft limits
- Conditions stable for duration of flight

Refer to BVLOS Operations Guide for specific requirements.

### 4.5 Weather Assessment

**Pre-Flight:**
- Check current conditions at site
- Review forecast for operation period
- Assess trends (improving/deteriorating)
- Check for convective activity potential
- Review any weather-related NOTAMs

**During Flight:**
- Continuously monitor conditions
- Be alert to changing weather
- Land before conditions deteriorate below limits

### 4.6 Weather Sources

Acceptable weather sources:
- Environment Canada forecasts
- Aviation weather (GFA, TAF, METAR for nearby aerodromes)
- On-site observation
- Reliable weather applications
- Client-provided weather monitoring

### 4.7 Wind Effects Considerations

| Effect | Consideration |
|:-------|:--------------|
| **Battery consumption** | Higher wind = faster battery drain |
| **Aircraft stability** | Small aircraft affected more by gusts |
| **Turbulence** | Near structures, terrain, treelines |
| **Lost link range** | Wind affects return-to-home capability |
| **Payload performance** | Gimbal stability, sensor quality |

### 4.8 Conditions Requiring Mission Abort

Abort the mission if:
- Wind exceeds limits
- Precipitation begins
- Visibility drops below minimum
- Thunderstorm approaches (within 30 km)
- Icing conditions develop
- Any condition that prevents safe operation

### 4.9 Documentation

Record on flight log:
- Weather conditions at start
- Any significant changes during flight
- Weather-related mission delays or aborts

## 5. Seasonal Considerations

### Summer (BC)
- Afternoon convection/thunderstorms
- High temperatures affecting batteries
- Smoke from wildfires affecting visibility
- Plan early morning flights when possible

### Winter
- Cold temperatures affecting batteries
- Snow/precipitation limitations
- Short daylight hours
- Ice/frost on aircraft

### Spring/Fall
- Variable conditions
- Fog potential
- Transition weather patterns
