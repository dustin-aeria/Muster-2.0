## 1. Introduction

This handbook serves as a quick reference guide for Pilots-in-Command operating under Aeria Solutions RPOC. It consolidates key information from various policies and procedures into a single field reference.

---

## 2. PIC Authority and Responsibility

### 2.1 You Are in Command

As PIC, you have:
- **Final authority** for safe operation of the flight
- **Responsibility** for compliance with regulations and procedures
- **Authority to refuse** any flight you believe is unsafe
- **Duty to stop** any operation that becomes unsafe

### 2.2 Key Responsibilities

| Area | Your Responsibility |
|:-----|:-------------------|
| **Safety** | Safe conduct of flight; crew safety |
| **Compliance** | Regulations, company procedures, authorizations |
| **Crew** | Brief, supervise, communicate with crew |
| **Aircraft** | Airworthiness verification; proper operation |
| **Documentation** | Complete all required documentation |
| **Reporting** | Report incidents and occurrences |

## 4. Flight Limits Quick Reference

### Altitude
| Situation | Limit |
|:----------|:------|
| Standard operations | 400 ft AGL |
| Near structures | May exceed per CAR 901.19 |
| Authorized (controlled airspace) | As specified |

### Distance
| From | Minimum |
|:-----|:--------|
| Uncontrolled aerodrome | 3 NM unless authorized |
| Controlled aerodrome | Authorization required |
| Emergency scene | 5 NM minimum |
| Bystanders (Basic) | 30m horizontal |
| Bystanders (Advanced) | Per conditions |

### Weather Limits
| Parameter | Standard Limit |
|:----------|:---------------|
| Wind sustained | ≤25 km/h |
| Gusts | ≤35 km/h |
| Visibility | ≥3 km |
| Precipitation | None (usually) |
| Thunderstorm | >30 km |

## 5. Emergency Quick Reference

### Lost Link
1. Wait for programmed behavior
2. Monitor telemetry
3. Attempt recovery
4. Clear landing area
5. Document and report

### Flyaway
1. Try RTH
2. Track position
3. Notify ATC if enters controlled airspace
4. Prepare recovery

### Battery Fire
1. Evacuate area
2. Use ABC extinguisher or sand
3. Do NOT use water
4. Monitor for re-ignition

### Traffic Conflict
1. Descend immediately
2. Move away
3. Give way to manned aircraft
4. Land if necessary

## 8. Go/No-Go Decision

### Go Criteria
All of these must be YES:
- [ ] Airspace clear/authorized
- [ ] Weather within limits
- [ ] Aircraft serviceable
- [ ] I am fit for duty
- [ ] Crew is ready
- [ ] Documentation complete

### No-Go Indicators
Any of these = NO-GO:
- Weather exceeds limits
- Aircraft defect
- Missing authorization
- Crew not fit/qualified
- Safety concern identified

**When in doubt, don't fly.**

## 9. Reporting Requirements

### Report Immediately
- Any injury
- Any property damage
- Any near mid-air collision
- Any flyaway
- Entry into controlled airspace (unauthorized)

### Report Same Day
- Equipment malfunction
- Lost link events
- Minor incidents

### Who to Report To
- Operations Manager: All incidents
- WorkSafeBC: Serious injury
- Transport Canada: Reportable occurrences

## 11. Daily Reminders

### Before Every Flight
- **Am I fit to fly?** (rested, alert, no impairment)
- **Is this flight safe?** (weather, site, equipment)
- **Do I have everything?** (documents, equipment, plan)
- **Is my crew ready?** (briefed, positioned, equipped)

### During Every Flight
- **Prioritize safety** over mission completion
- **Maintain situational awareness**
- **Communicate with crew**
- **Be prepared to abort**

### After Every Flight
- **Complete documentation**
- **Report any issues**
- **Secure equipment**
- **Learn from the experience**
