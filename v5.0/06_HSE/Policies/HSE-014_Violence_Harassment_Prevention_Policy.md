## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes Aeria Solutions' commitment to providing a workplace free from violence, harassment, discrimination, and bullying. All workers have the right to a respectful and safe work environment.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions personnel
- Contractors, clients, and visitors
- All workplaces including field sites, client locations, and company events
- All work-related interactions including digital communications

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Violence** | Physical force or attempted force that causes or could cause injury; threats of violence |
| **Harassment** | Conduct that demeans, humiliates, or embarrasses; unwelcome conduct affecting dignity |
| **Sexual Harassment** | Unwelcome sexual conduct, advances, requests, or comments |
| **Bullying** | Repeated, unreasonable behavior directed at a worker that creates risk to health and safety |
| **Discrimination** | Adverse treatment based on protected grounds under BC Human Rights Code |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 Zero Tolerance

Aeria Solutions has zero tolerance for:
- Workplace violence or threats
- Harassment of any kind
- Sexual harassment
- Bullying behavior
- Discrimination on any protected ground

### 5.2 Protected Grounds

Discrimination or harassment based on the following grounds is prohibited:
- Race, colour, ancestry, place of origin
- Religion
- Marital or family status
- Physical or mental disability
- Sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression
- Age
- Political belief
- Criminal or summary conviction unrelated to employment

### 5.3 Examples of Prohibited Conduct

| Category | Examples |
|:---------|:---------|
| **Violence** | Physical assault, pushing, threats, intimidation, damaging property |
| **Harassment** | Offensive jokes, slurs, name-calling, displaying offensive material, exclusion |
| **Sexual Harassment** | Unwelcome touching, comments about appearance, requests for sexual favors |
| **Bullying** | Repeated criticism, spreading rumors, undermining work, isolating someone |

### 5.4 Reporting

Workers who experience or witness prohibited conduct shall:
1. Tell the person to stop (if safe and comfortable doing so)
2. Document the incident(s)
3. Report to their supervisor, Operations Manager, or Accountable Executive
4. Contact WorkSafeBC if internal process inadequate

Reports may be made:
- Verbally or in writing
- Anonymously (though this limits investigation)
- Without fear of reprisal

### 5.5 Investigation

All reports will be:
- Taken seriously
- Investigated promptly and impartially
- Kept confidential to extent possible
- Concluded with appropriate action

Investigation may involve:
- Interviewing parties and witnesses
- Reviewing documentation
- Assessing credibility
- Determining findings and actions

### 5.6 No Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports or participates in an investigation is prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.

### 5.7 Consequences

Violations may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Required training
- Suspension
- Termination
- Denial of site access (contractors)
- Legal action (criminal conduct)

### 5.8 Support

Workers who experience violence or harassment will be:
- Supported with accommodations as needed
- Referred to resources (EAP, counseling)
- Protected from further exposure to the perpetrator
- Not required to work with the perpetrator during investigation

### 5.9 Risk Assessment

Aeria Solutions will assess risks of workplace violence considering:
- Nature of work and locations
- Interaction with public
- Working alone
- Past incidents
- Client or site-specific risks

Controls will be implemented based on assessment.
