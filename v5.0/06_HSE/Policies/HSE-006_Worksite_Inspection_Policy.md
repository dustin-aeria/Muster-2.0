## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for regular workplace inspections to identify hazards, verify control effectiveness, and ensure compliance with health and safety requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions worksites and operations
- Company facilities, equipment, and vehicles
- Field operations and client sites
- All personnel conducting inspections

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Inspection Types

| Type | Frequency | Who | Scope |
|:-----|:----------|:----|:------|
| **Pre-Use Equipment** | Before each use | Operator/PIC | Specific equipment being used |
| **Daily Site** | Each work day | PIC | Active worksite (part of FLHA) |
| **Vehicle** | Weekly / before trips | Driver | Company vehicles |
| **Formal Workplace** | Monthly | Operations Manager | Facilities, general conditions |
| **Management Review** | Quarterly | Accountable Executive | System-wide review |

### 4.2 Pre-Use Equipment Inspections

Before each use, operators shall inspect:
- RPAS equipment per MCM checklists
- Ground support equipment
- PPE condition
- Tools and accessories

**See:** MCM-003-PR Pre-Flight Inspection Procedure

### 4.3 Daily Site Inspections

PICs shall conduct site inspections as part of the FLHA process:
- Walk the site perimeter
- Identify new or changed hazards
- Verify emergency equipment is accessible
- Check weather and environmental conditions
- Document findings on FLHA form

### 4.4 Formal Inspections

Monthly formal inspections shall:
- Use standardized inspection checklists (FRM-INSP)
- Cover all areas of the workplace
- Check equipment condition
- Verify safety equipment (first aid, fire extinguishers, PPE)
- Review housekeeping and storage
- Identify deficiencies and corrective actions

### 4.5 Inspection Requirements

All inspections shall:
1. Be conducted by trained personnel
2. Use appropriate checklists
3. Be documented
4. Identify corrective actions for deficiencies
5. Track corrective action completion
6. Be retained per record retention requirements

### 4.6 Deficiency Management

| Deficiency Level | Response Time | Action Required |
|:-----------------|:--------------|:----------------|
| **Critical** (imminent danger) | Immediate | Stop work; correct before proceeding |
| **Major** (significant hazard) | 24 hours | Priority correction; interim controls |
| **Minor** (low risk) | 7 days | Scheduled correction |

### 4.7 Documentation

Inspection records shall include:
- Date and time of inspection
- Inspector name
- Areas/equipment inspected
- Items checked
- Deficiencies found
- Corrective actions assigned
- Completion verification
