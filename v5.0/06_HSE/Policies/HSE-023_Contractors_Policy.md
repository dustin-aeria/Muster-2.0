## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish requirements for the safe management of contractors and subcontractors engaged by Aeria Solutions Ltd. Contractors are integral to the company's operations, providing specialized services and supporting RPAS activities across diverse project types. This policy ensures that contractors work safely, meet company standards, and integrate effectively with Aeria Solutions Ltd personnel.

This policy supports SECOR certification requirements for contractor management and ensures compliance with WorkSafeBC prime contractor obligations and multi-employer worksite requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All contractors and subcontractors engaged by Aeria Solutions Ltd
- All company personnel who hire, supervise, or work alongside contractors
- All worksites where contractors perform work on behalf of the company
- All projects where Aeria Solutions Ltd acts as prime contractor

**Types of contractors covered:**

| Contractor Type | Examples |
|:----------------|:---------|
| **Specialized Service Providers** | Surveyors, data processors, equipment technicians |
| **Professional Consultants** | Engineers, environmental consultants, legal advisors |
| **Equipment/Labor Suppliers** | Equipment rental, temporary labor services |
| **Subcontracted RPAS Operators** | Pilots and crew from other operators |
| **Maintenance Providers** | Vehicle maintenance, equipment repair services |
| **Facility Services** | Cleaning, security, IT support |

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to ensuring that all contractors working on behalf of the company operate safely and in compliance with applicable regulations, company policies, and client requirements. Contractors are expected to meet the same safety standards as company employees.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Pre-Qualification:** All contractors shall be pre-qualified to verify their competence, safety performance, and regulatory compliance before engagement.

2. **Clear Communication:** Contractors shall receive clear information about hazards, expectations, and requirements before commencing work.

3. **Integrated Safety:** Contractors shall be integrated into site safety programs and participate in safety activities alongside company personnel.

4. **Ongoing Oversight:** Company personnel shall monitor contractor performance and intervene if safety standards are not maintained.

5. **Shared Responsibility:** While contractors are responsible for their own safety programs, Aeria Solutions Ltd retains accountability for the safe coordination of all work activities.

6. **Continuous Improvement:** Contractor safety performance shall be evaluated and factored into future engagement decisions.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Contractor** | An individual or company engaged under contract to provide services or labor, who is not an employee of Aeria Solutions Ltd |
| **Prime Contractor** | The employer or contractor designated to coordinate health and safety activities at a multiple-employer worksite, as defined by WorkSafeBC |
| **Subcontractor** | A contractor engaged by another contractor to perform part of the contracted work |
| **Pre-Qualification** | The process of evaluating a contractor's capabilities, safety performance, and compliance before engagement |
| **Site Orientation** | Training provided to contractors before they begin work, covering site-specific hazards, rules, and emergency procedures |
| **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** | A contract establishing the general terms and conditions for contractor services, which may be supplemented by project-specific work orders |
| **Competent Worker** | A worker who is qualified because of knowledge, training, and experience to perform assigned work safely |
| **TRIR (Total Recordable Incident Rate)** | A standardized measure of workplace injuries calculated as (Number of recordable injuries x 200,000) / Total hours worked |

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Pre-Qualification Requirements

**Pre-Qualification Process:**

Before engaging any contractor, the following shall be verified:

| Requirement | Documentation |
|:------------|:--------------|
| **Business Registration** | Valid business license, corporate registration |
| **WorkSafeBC Registration** | Active WorkSafeBC account in good standing, clearance letter |
| **Insurance Coverage** | Certificate of insurance meeting minimum requirements |
| **Safety Program** | Written health and safety program appropriate to their work |
| **Training Records** | Certificates for required training and certifications |
| **Safety Statistics** | TRIR, lost-time incidents, and fatalities for past 3 years |
| **References** | Contact information for at least 2 recent clients |
| **Certifications** | Industry certifications (COR, SECOR, ISO, etc.) if applicable |

**Insurance Requirements:**

Contractors shall maintain minimum insurance coverage:

| Coverage Type | Minimum Amount |
|:--------------|:---------------|
| **Commercial General Liability** | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| **Professional Liability** (if applicable) | $1,000,000 per occurrence |
| **Automobile Liability** | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| **Aviation Liability** (RPAS contractors) | $100,000 per occurrence |

Aeria Solutions Ltd shall be named as additional insured on liability policies.

**Safety Performance Criteria:**

Contractors should demonstrate:

- TRIR below industry average for their sector
- No fatalities in the past 5 years
- No WorkSafeBC orders or penalties in the past 3 years
- Commitment to continuous safety improvement

**Pre-Qualification Approval:**

- Pre-qualification packages reviewed by Operations Manager
- Approved contractors added to Approved Contractor List
- Pre-qualification valid for 12 months, subject to annual renewal
- Contractors may be removed for poor performance or non-compliance

### 5.2 Site Orientation

**Orientation Requirements:**

All contractors shall complete site orientation before beginning work:

**General Orientation (all contractors):**

- Company safety policies and expectations
- Hazard reporting procedures
- Emergency response and evacuation procedures
- PPE requirements
- Drug and alcohol policy
- Incident reporting requirements
- Prohibited activities and consequences
- Contact information for company representatives

**Site-Specific Orientation (for each worksite):**

- Site-specific hazards and controls
- Site layout and restricted areas
- Emergency assembly points
- Site-specific rules and client requirements
- Coordination with other contractors or workers
- Communication protocols
- Equipment and material storage areas

**Orientation Documentation:**

- Contractors shall sign orientation attendance records
- Records retained for duration of contract plus 3 years
- Re-orientation required if contractor absent from site more than 30 days
- Update orientation when significant hazards or procedures change

**Remote Project Orientation:**

For remote or distributed projects:

- Pre-job briefings conducted by phone or video conference
- Written orientation materials provided in advance
- Acknowledgment of receipt and understanding required
- In-person site orientation upon arrival at worksite

### 5.3 Supervision Requirements

**Company Supervision:**

Company personnel shall provide appropriate oversight of contractor work:

| Supervision Level | Application |
|:------------------|:------------|
| **Direct Supervision** | New contractors, high-risk activities, performance concerns |
| **Regular Monitoring** | Ongoing work by established contractors |
| **Periodic Checks** | Routine services by long-term contractors with good records |
| **Audit-Based** | Specialized services subject to periodic audits |

**Contractor Supervision:**

Contractors shall:

- Provide competent supervisors for their work crews
- Ensure supervisor present when workers are on site
- Empower supervisors to stop work for safety concerns
- Communicate supervisor contact information to company

**Company Representative:**

A company representative shall be designated for each contractor engagement:

- Primary point of contact for contractor
- Authority to direct work within contract scope
- Authority to stop work for safety concerns
- Responsible for monitoring contractor performance
- Documents contractor performance and issues

### 5.4 Coordination Requirements

**Prime Contractor Obligations:**

When Aeria Solutions Ltd is the prime contractor:

- Establish site-specific health and safety rules
- Coordinate health and safety activities of all employers
- Conduct or arrange regular safety inspections
- Ensure first aid services are available
- Post prime contractor notice at worksite
- Ensure all contractors are oriented to site hazards

**Multiple Contractor Coordination:**

When multiple contractors work at the same site:

- Pre-job meetings to discuss coordination requirements
- Clear delineation of work areas and responsibilities
- Communication protocols for hazard identification
- Coordination of high-risk activities (hot work, confined space, etc.)
- Shared emergency response procedures

**Client Site Coordination:**

When working at client facilities:

- Comply with client contractor management requirements
- Participate in client orientation and safety programs
- Coordinate with client safety representatives
- Follow client work permit systems if applicable
- Report to client any incidents or hazards identified

**Communication:**

Effective communication shall be maintained through:

- Daily pre-job briefings for changing work activities
- Regular progress meetings for extended projects
- Immediate communication of hazards or incidents
- Clear chain of command and contact information

### 5.5 Incident Reporting

**Contractor Reporting Obligations:**

Contractors shall immediately report to the company representative:

- All injuries, regardless of severity
- All near-misses with significant potential consequences
- All property damage or equipment damage
- All environmental incidents
- Any regulatory inspector visits or enforcement actions
- Any conditions that could affect safe completion of work

**Reporting Timelines:**

| Incident Type | Reporting Requirement |
|:--------------|:---------------------|
| **Serious injury or fatality** | Immediately by phone |
| **Medical treatment injury** | Same day by phone |
| **First aid injury** | End of shift, documented |
| **Near-miss (serious potential)** | Same day by phone |
| **Property/equipment damage** | Same day by phone |
| **Environmental incident** | Immediately by phone |

**Company Response:**

Upon receiving incident reports:

- Company representative to attend site for serious incidents
- Joint investigation conducted with contractor
- Corrective actions identified and tracked
- Lessons learned shared with relevant personnel
- Incident factored into contractor performance evaluation

**Regulatory Reporting:**

The company shall coordinate with contractors on regulatory reporting:

- WorkSafeBC reporting for serious incidents
- Transport Canada reporting for RPAS incidents
- Environmental reporting as required
- Ensure contractor reports do not conflict with company reports

### 5.6 Performance Evaluation

**Ongoing Evaluation:**

Contractor performance shall be monitored through:

- Safety inspection results
- Incident and near-miss frequency
- Compliance with safety requirements
- Quality of work delivered
- Timeliness and reliability
- Professionalism and communication

**Post-Contract Evaluation:**

Upon completion of significant contracts:

- Formal performance evaluation completed
- Evaluation shared with contractor
- Results inform future engagement decisions
- Issues requiring corrective action documented

**Performance Issues:**

Poor safety performance may result in:

| Issue | Response |
|:------|:---------|
| **Minor non-compliance** | Verbal warning, documented, monitored |
| **Repeated non-compliance** | Written warning, corrective action required |
| **Serious non-compliance** | Work stoppage, formal review, possible removal |
| **Imminent danger** | Immediate removal from site |
| **Pattern of issues** | Removal from Approved Contractor List |

**Recognition:**

Contractors demonstrating excellent safety performance may be:

- Recognized in company communications
- Given preference for future work
- Invited to participate in safety initiatives
- Considered for longer-term agreements
