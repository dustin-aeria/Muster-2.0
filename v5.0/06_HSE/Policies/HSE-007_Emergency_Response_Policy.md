## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for emergency preparedness and response to protect workers, the public, and property during emergency situations.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions operations and worksites
- All personnel, contractors, and visitors
- All types of emergencies (medical, environmental, operational, security)

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Emergency Types

Aeria Solutions shall be prepared for the following emergencies:

| Category | Examples |
|:---------|:---------|
| **Medical** | Injury, illness, cardiac event, heat/cold stress |
| **Environmental** | Fire, severe weather, wildlife encounter, natural disaster |
| **Operational** | RPAS flyaway, crash, battery fire, equipment failure |
| **Security** | Hostile person, theft, unauthorized access |
| **Transportation** | Vehicle accident, equipment failure during travel |

### 4.2 Emergency Preparedness

1. **Planning:** Emergency response procedures shall be developed for all foreseeable emergencies.

2. **Communication:** Emergency contact information shall be:
   - Current and accessible
   - Communicated during orientation and briefings
   - Posted at fixed worksites
   - Available at field sites

3. **Equipment:** Emergency equipment shall be:
   - Appropriate for the hazards present
   - Readily accessible
   - Regularly inspected
   - Maintained in working condition

4. **Training:** Workers shall be trained in:
   - Emergency procedures relevant to their work
   - Use of emergency equipment
   - First aid (designated personnel)
   - Emergency roles and responsibilities

### 4.3 Emergency Response Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|:------------|:------------|
| **Immediate Response** | Protect life, prevent further harm |
| **Communication** | Alert others, call emergency services as needed |
| **Assembly** | Account for all personnel |
| **Reporting** | Notify management; report per incident requirements |
| **Investigation** | Determine causes; implement improvements |

### 4.4 Site-Specific Requirements

Before work begins at any site, the PIC shall:
1. Identify nearest emergency services (hospital, fire, police)
2. Determine communication methods (cell coverage, radio)
3. Identify evacuation routes and assembly points
4. Ensure first aid supplies are available
5. Brief crew on emergency procedures
6. Document emergency information on FLHA

### 4.5 Remote Location Operations

For operations in remote locations:
- Emergency communication plan required
- Enhanced first aid provisions
- Evacuation plan considering terrain and access
- Emergency locator device if applicable
- Emergency shelter provisions if warranted

### 4.6 RPAS-Specific Emergencies

| Emergency | Response |
|:----------|:---------|
| **Flyaway** | Initiate lost link procedure; track; notify as required |
| **Crash** | Secure area; assess injuries; preserve evidence; report |
| **Battery Fire** | Use appropriate suppression; evacuate area; monitor for re-ignition |
| **Near Mid-Air** | Land immediately; report per Transport Canada requirements |

**See:** OPS-011-PR Emergency Procedures (RPAS)

### 4.7 Drills and Exercises

- Emergency drills shall be conducted at least annually
- Drill results shall be documented
- Lessons learned shall be incorporated into procedures
