## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for health and safety training to ensure all workers have the knowledge, skills, and competency to work safely. This policy complements TCP-001 (Training & Competency Program Manual) with focus on HSE-specific requirements.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions employees
- Contractors and subcontractors
- New hires, transfers, and returning workers
- Any person working under Aeria direction

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 General Requirements

1. **No worker shall perform a task until they have received adequate training** for that task.

2. Training shall ensure workers can:
   - Recognize hazards in their work
   - Understand and apply controls
   - Perform tasks safely
   - Respond to emergencies

3. Training shall be provided:
   - Before starting work (orientation)
   - When tasks or conditions change
   - When new hazards are introduced
   - As refresher training at defined intervals

### 4.2 Orientation Training

All new workers shall complete orientation before starting work, including:

| Topic | Content |
|:------|:--------|
| Health & Safety Policy | HSE-001 review and commitment |
| Worker Rights & Responsibilities | HSE-002 review |
| Hazard Assessment | How FLHAs work; worker participation |
| Hazard Reporting | How to report hazards and incidents |
| Emergency Procedures | Site-specific emergency response |
| PPE Requirements | Required equipment and proper use |
| Refuse Unsafe Work | Right to refuse and procedure |
| First Aid | Location of first aid, who to contact |
| Key Contacts | Supervisors, safety contacts |

**See:** TCP-001-PR New Personnel Orientation Procedure

### 4.3 Task-Specific Training

Workers shall receive specific training for:
- Each task they are assigned
- Equipment they will operate
- Hazards specific to their work
- Required PPE and its proper use
- Applicable safe work procedures

Training shall include practical demonstration of competency, not just information transfer.

### 4.4 Site-Specific Training

Before working at any new site, workers shall be oriented to:
- Site-specific hazards
- Site emergency procedures
- Site rules and requirements
- Client safety requirements (if applicable)
- Location of emergency equipment

### 4.5 Supervisor Training

Supervisors (including PICs) shall receive additional training in:
- Hazard recognition and control
- Conducting effective briefings
- Addressing unsafe conditions and behaviors
- Incident response and reporting
- Worker rights and employer duties

### 4.6 Competency Verification

1. Training shall include verification that workers:
   - Understand the information provided
   - Can perform tasks safely
   - Know what to do in emergencies

2. Competency may be verified through:
   - Practical demonstration
   - Written or verbal assessment
   - Observation during work
   - Sign-off by trainer or supervisor

### 4.7 Training Records

Training records shall include:
- Worker name
- Training topic and content
- Date and duration
- Trainer name and qualifications
- Competency verification method
- Worker signature

Records shall be retained per ADM-001-PR Record Retention Procedure.

### 4.8 Refresher Training

Refresher training shall be provided:
- As specified in FHAs or procedures
- Following incidents or near-misses
- When performance deficiencies are observed
- At intervals defined in TCP-001

## 5. Minimum HSE Training Requirements

| Training | Who | When | Refresher |
|:---------|:----|:-----|:----------|
| Orientation | All workers | Before starting work | — |
| FLHA Process | All field workers | Before first field assignment | Annual |
| Emergency Response | All workers | During orientation | Annual |
| First Aid | Designated first aiders | Before designation | Per certification |
| PPE Use | All workers | Before use | As needed |
| Hazard Reporting | All workers | During orientation | Annual |
| WHMIS | Workers handling hazardous materials | Before handling | 3 years |
