## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for managing fatigue as a workplace hazard to ensure workers are fit for duty and can perform safety-sensitive tasks safely. Fatigue impairs judgment, reaction time, and decision-making—critical skills for RPAS operations.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions personnel
- All operations, especially safety-sensitive duties
- Pilots-in-Command, Visual Observers, and ground crew
- Drivers operating company vehicles

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Fatigue** | Physical and/or mental impairment from insufficient rest, extended wakefulness, or circadian disruption |
| **Duty Period** | Time from reporting for duty until released from duty |
| **Rest Period** | Time off duty for sleep and recovery |
| **Safety-Sensitive** | Duties where impairment poses risk to safety (flying, driving, operating equipment) |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 Fatigue as a Hazard

1. Fatigue is recognized as a significant hazard that can impair performance.
2. Fatigue risk shall be managed like any other workplace hazard.
3. Workers shall not perform safety-sensitive duties when fatigued to the point of impairment.

### 5.2 Flight Crew Duty Limits

While CARs Part VII flight duty limits do not directly apply to RPAS, Aeria adopts conservative limits as best practice:

| Limit Type | Maximum |
|:-----------|:--------|
| **Flight Duty Period** | 14 hours (single pilot) |
| **Actual Flight Time** | 8 hours in any 24-hour period |
| **Consecutive Duty Days** | 7 days without a day off |
| **Minimum Rest Period** | 10 consecutive hours between duty periods |
| **Minimum Sleep Opportunity** | 8 hours within each rest period |

For complex operations or high-workload situations, these limits should be reduced.

### 5.3 Driving Limits

| Limit Type | Maximum |
|:-----------|:--------|
| **Driving Time** | 10 hours in a day |
| **On-Duty Time** | 14 hours per day |
| **Consecutive Driving Days** | 7 days |
| **Rest After Driving** | 8 hours minimum before driving again |

For combined flight and driving duty, total duty shall not exceed 14 hours.

### 5.4 Worker Responsibilities

Workers shall:
- Arrive for duty adequately rested
- Report when feeling too fatigued to work safely
- Plan for adequate sleep before duty
- Not consume alcohol within 8 hours of duty
- Report sleep disorders or conditions affecting rest

### 5.5 Employer Responsibilities

Aeria Solutions shall:
- Schedule work to allow adequate rest
- Not require workers to exceed duty limits
- Support workers who report fatigue
- Monitor for signs of fatigue
- Consider fatigue in incident investigations

### 5.6 Managing Fatigue Risk

| Situation | Action |
|:----------|:-------|
| **Extended duty anticipated** | Plan for relief or shift handover |
| **Worker reports fatigue** | Assess fitness; adjust duties or allow rest |
| **Signs of fatigue observed** | Discuss with worker; consider stopping work |
| **Multiple early starts or late finishes** | Schedule recovery day |
| **Long driving distances** | Plan rest stops; consider overnight stay |

### 5.7 Signs of Fatigue

- Excessive yawning
- Heavy eyelids, difficulty keeping eyes open
- Delayed reactions
- Poor concentration or memory
- Irritability or mood changes
- Microsleeps
- Lack of motivation

### 5.8 Reporting and Documentation

- Fatigue concerns may be reported without reprisal
- Fatigue should be documented on FLHAs when relevant
- Fatigue-related incidents shall be investigated for root causes
