## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish Aeria Solutions Ltd's commitment to maintaining a drug and alcohol-free workplace that ensures the safety and well-being of all employees, contractors, clients, and the public. RPAS operations require the highest levels of alertness, judgment, and coordination, making impairment from drugs or alcohol an unacceptable safety risk.

This policy provides a clear framework for preventing substance impairment in the workplace while respecting the privacy, dignity, and rights of all personnel. It also establishes support mechanisms for those who may be struggling with substance use issues.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Aeria Solutions Ltd employees, regardless of position or tenure
- All contractors and subcontractors working on behalf of Aeria Solutions Ltd
- All persons on company premises or worksites
- All persons operating company equipment, including vehicles and RPAS
- All personnel representing the company at client sites or public events

This policy is in effect:

- During all working hours, including overtime
- When on call or standby duty
- When operating company vehicles or equipment
- When representing the company at any function
- When on company premises, including parking areas
- When at client sites or remote field locations

This policy covers:

- Alcohol (beverage alcohol of any type)
- Cannabis and cannabis products
- Illegal drugs and controlled substances
- Prescription medications that may cause impairment
- Over-the-counter medications that may cause impairment
- Any substance that impairs judgment, coordination, or alertness

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd maintains a **zero tolerance policy** for impairment in the workplace. The company is committed to providing a safe working environment free from the risks associated with drug and alcohol impairment.

**Core Principles:**

1. **No employee or contractor shall report to work or remain at work while impaired** by alcohol, drugs, or any other substance that affects their ability to work safely.

2. **No employee or contractor shall use, possess, distribute, or sell** alcohol, illegal drugs, or controlled substances on company premises, worksites, or in company vehicles.

3. **No employee or contractor shall operate any company equipment, vehicle, or RPAS** while under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or any impairing substance.

4. **All employees are responsible for ensuring their fitness for duty** before commencing work activities.

5. **The company will provide support and accommodation** for employees who voluntarily seek help for substance use issues, while maintaining safety as the primary concern.

This policy is designed to protect all personnel while complying with applicable human rights legislation, privacy laws, and occupational health and safety regulations.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Alcohol** | Any beverage or product containing ethyl alcohol, including beer, wine, spirits, and alcohol-infused products |
| **Cannabis** | Marijuana, hashish, THC, and any cannabis-derived products, regardless of method of consumption or legal status |
| **Controlled Substance** | Any drug or substance whose possession, use, or distribution is regulated under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act |
| **Drug** | Any substance, legal or illegal, that affects the mind or body, including prescription medications, over-the-counter drugs, cannabis, and illicit substances |
| **Fit for Duty** | Being in a physical, mental, and emotional state that enables an employee to perform their job safely and effectively, without impairment from any substance |
| **Impairment** | A state where a person's ability to safely perform their job duties is diminished by the effects of alcohol, drugs, or other substances |
| **Prescription Medication** | A drug that requires a prescription from a licensed healthcare provider and may cause impairment as a side effect |
| **Reasonable Cause** | Observable, objective indicators that an employee may be impaired, such as slurred speech, impaired coordination, or erratic behavior |
| **Safety-Sensitive Position** | A position where impairment could result in direct and significant risk of injury to the worker, others, or the environment; all RPAS operational positions are considered safety-sensitive |

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Zero Tolerance Standards

**Prohibited Conduct:**

The following conduct is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination:

- Reporting to work or remaining at work while impaired by any substance
- Using, possessing, or distributing alcohol on company premises or worksites
- Using, possessing, or distributing illegal drugs or controlled substances
- Consuming alcohol within eight (8) hours of a scheduled shift
- Using cannabis within twenty-four (24) hours of a scheduled shift
- Operating company vehicles or equipment while impaired
- Refusing to cooperate with testing when reasonable cause exists
- Tampering with or attempting to falsify test results

**Fitness for Duty:**

All personnel must:

- Report to work fit for duty, free from impairment
- Remain fit for duty throughout their shift
- Not consume alcohol or cannabis during working hours, including breaks
- Ensure adequate time has elapsed between substance use and work
- Report any concerns about their fitness for duty to their supervisor

### 5.2 Testing Situations

Testing may be conducted in the following circumstances, in accordance with applicable law and with respect for employee rights:

**Post-Incident Testing:**

- Testing may be required following any significant workplace incident
- Incidents triggering testing include vehicle collisions, equipment damage, injuries, or near-misses with serious potential consequences
- Testing must be conducted as soon as reasonably practical following the incident

**Reasonable Cause Testing:**

- Testing may be required when a supervisor has reasonable cause to believe an employee is impaired
- Reasonable cause must be based on specific, observable indicators
- A second supervisor or witness should confirm observations where possible
- Observations must be documented using the Reasonable Cause Documentation Form

**Pre-Employment Testing:**

- Pre-employment testing may be conducted for safety-sensitive positions
- Offers of employment may be conditional upon passing pre-employment testing
- Testing will be conducted in accordance with human rights legislation

**Return to Duty Testing:**

- Testing may be required as a condition of return to work following a violation
- Return to duty testing will be part of a comprehensive return to work agreement

**Testing Procedures:**

All testing shall be:

- Conducted by qualified, accredited testing providers
- Performed using chain-of-custody procedures
- Respectful of employee privacy and dignity
- Documented and records maintained confidentially
- Subject to confirmation testing before any adverse action

### 5.3 Prescription and Over-the-Counter Medications

**Disclosure Requirements:**

Employees taking medications that may cause impairment shall:

- Consult with their healthcare provider about potential workplace effects
- Disclose to their supervisor if medication may affect fitness for duty
- Provide medical documentation confirming fitness for specific duties if requested
- Not operate vehicles, equipment, or RPAS if medication causes impairment

**Accommodation:**

The company will:

- Work with employees to accommodate legitimate medical needs
- Consider modified duties or adjusted schedules where appropriate
- Maintain confidentiality of medical information
- Not penalize employees for proper disclosure of medication use

**Prohibited Conduct:**

Employees shall not:

- Use prescription medications other than as prescribed
- Share prescription medications with others
- Operate safety-sensitive equipment if medication causes impairment
- Fail to disclose medication that may affect safety-sensitive duties

### 5.4 Impairment Recognition and Reporting

**Indicators of Impairment:**

Supervisors and workers shall be alert to signs of possible impairment, including:

- Slurred or incoherent speech
- Unsteady gait or impaired coordination
- Bloodshot or glassy eyes
- Unusual behavior or mood changes
- Smell of alcohol or cannabis
- Decreased concentration or alertness
- Tremors or excessive sweating
- Disorientation or confusion

**Self-Reporting:**

Employees who recognize they may not be fit for duty shall:

- Immediately inform their supervisor
- Remove themselves from safety-sensitive duties
- Cooperate with arrangements for safe transportation home
- Not face discipline for voluntary disclosure (unless policy violated)

**Reporting by Others:**

All personnel have a duty to report:

- Observed impairment or unfitness for duty in others
- Possession or use of prohibited substances in the workplace
- Concerns about a colleague's ability to work safely

Reports may be made to:

- The employee's supervisor
- The Operations Manager
- The Accountable Executive
- Anonymously through the company's reporting system

### 5.5 Consequences of Policy Violations

**First Violation:**

A first violation of this policy may result in:

- Immediate removal from the worksite
- Formal written warning
- Mandatory participation in substance abuse assessment
- Return to work agreement with monitoring conditions
- Additional training requirements

**Second Violation:**

A second violation of this policy will result in:

- Immediate termination of employment

**Serious Violations:**

The following may result in immediate termination without prior warning:

- Distribution or sale of illegal substances
- Impairment resulting in injury or significant property damage
- Refusal to cooperate with testing
- Falsification of test results
- Criminal conduct related to substance use

**Contractor Violations:**

Contractors who violate this policy:

- Will be immediately removed from the worksite
- May be permanently barred from company worksites
- May have their contract terminated
- Will be reported to their employer

### 5.6 Support Resources

Aeria Solutions Ltd recognizes that substance use disorders are medical conditions and encourages employees to seek help.

**Available Support:**

- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Confidential counseling and referral services available 24/7
- **Benefits Coverage:** Extended health benefits may cover substance abuse treatment
- **Medical Leave:** Leave may be available for employees undergoing treatment
- **Accommodation:** The company will accommodate employees undergoing treatment in accordance with human rights legislation

**Voluntary Disclosure:**

Employees who voluntarily disclose a substance use problem before a policy violation:

- Will be offered assistance and support
- Will not face discipline solely for the disclosure
- Will be supported in accessing treatment
- Must comply with fitness for duty requirements

**Return to Work:**

Employees returning from treatment may be subject to:

- Last-chance agreement with specific conditions
- Return to duty testing
- Ongoing monitoring and follow-up testing
- Modified duties during transition period

**Confidentiality:**

All information regarding substance use issues and treatment will be:

- Maintained in strict confidence
- Shared only with those who need to know
- Stored separately from personnel files
- Protected in accordance with privacy legislation
