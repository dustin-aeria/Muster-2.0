## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish comprehensive requirements for the safe operation of vehicles used by Aeria Solutions Ltd personnel during RPAS operations and related activities. This policy ensures the protection of employees, contractors, the public, and company assets through the implementation of safe driving practices, proper vehicle maintenance, and compliance with all applicable transportation regulations.

Vehicle incidents represent a significant risk in field-based RPAS operations, where personnel frequently travel to remote or challenging locations. This policy provides the framework necessary to minimize vehicle-related incidents and ensure all personnel arrive safely at their destinations.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Aeria Solutions Ltd employees and contractors operating company-owned vehicles
- All personnel operating personal vehicles for company business
- All personnel operating rental vehicles for company purposes
- All vehicles used for transporting RPAS equipment, personnel, or materials
- All driving activities conducted during work hours or in the course of employment
- All provinces and territories where Aeria Solutions Ltd operates, with primary focus on British Columbia

This policy covers:

- Light-duty vehicles (cars, SUVs, pickup trucks)
- Medium-duty vehicles used for equipment transport
- Utility vehicles including ATVs and UTVs where applicable
- Towing operations including trailers and equipment transport
- Winter driving conditions common to BC operations

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to ensuring the safety of all personnel during vehicle operations. The company recognizes that driving is one of the highest-risk activities our employees undertake and maintains a zero-tolerance policy for unsafe driving practices.

All personnel shall:

- Operate vehicles in a safe, courteous, and legally compliant manner at all times
- Ensure vehicles are properly maintained and inspected before use
- Plan journeys appropriately, accounting for weather, road conditions, and fatigue
- Never operate a vehicle while impaired by alcohol, drugs, fatigue, or distraction
- Report all vehicle incidents, near-misses, and mechanical defects immediately
- Comply with all applicable federal, provincial, and local traffic laws

The company shall provide appropriate vehicles, training, and resources to support safe vehicle operations across all RPAS activities.

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Driver Qualifications

All personnel operating vehicles for Aeria Solutions Ltd shall:

- Hold a valid driver's license appropriate for the class of vehicle being operated
- Maintain a clean driving record with no more than six (6) demerit points
- Provide a current driver's abstract upon hire and annually thereafter
- Complete company-approved defensive driving training within 90 days of hire
- Complete winter driving orientation before operating in winter conditions
- Report any license suspensions, restrictions, or violations immediately to management
- Disclose any medical conditions that may affect driving ability
- Be at least 21 years of age to operate company vehicles; 25 years for towing operations

### 5.2 Vehicle Inspections

**Pre-Trip Inspections:**

Before operating any vehicle, drivers shall conduct and document a pre-trip inspection including:

- Tires: condition, pressure, and tread depth (minimum 3/32")
- Lights: headlights, taillights, brake lights, turn signals, hazard lights
- Mirrors: adjustment and condition
- Windshield and windows: cleanliness and freedom from cracks
- Wipers and washer fluid: operation and adequate fluid levels
- Brakes: pedal feel and parking brake function
- Horn: operation
- Fuel level: adequate for planned journey plus reserve
- Emergency equipment: first aid kit, fire extinguisher, warning triangles
- Vehicle documents: registration, insurance, operator's manual

**Post-Trip Inspections:**

Upon completion of each trip, drivers shall:

- Note any defects or concerns observed during operation
- Report any damage, unusual sounds, or warning lights
- Ensure the vehicle is secured and properly parked
- Complete the vehicle log with mileage and any issues noted

**Periodic Inspections:**

Company vehicles shall undergo:

- Monthly safety inspections by the assigned driver
- Manufacturer-recommended maintenance at scheduled intervals
- Annual commercial vehicle inspections where required by regulation

### 5.3 Load Securing

All loads transported in company vehicles shall be:

- Secured using appropriate tie-downs, straps, or containment devices
- Distributed evenly to maintain vehicle stability and handling
- Within the vehicle's Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR)
- Protected from weather and road debris as appropriate
- Inspected before departure and at each stop

**RPAS Equipment Transport:**

- RPAS equipment shall be transported in protective cases or containers
- Batteries shall be transported in accordance with Transport Canada regulations
- Sensitive equipment shall be secured to prevent movement and damage
- Equipment shall be verified complete before departure and upon arrival

**Cargo Weight Limits:**

- Never exceed the manufacturer's payload capacity
- Account for passenger weight when calculating load limits
- Distribute weight appropriately between axles

### 5.4 Driving Conditions Assessment

**Pre-Journey Planning:**

Before each journey, drivers shall:

- Check current and forecasted weather conditions
- Assess road conditions along the planned route
- Identify alternative routes if conditions deteriorate
- Estimate travel time including appropriate rest breaks
- Communicate journey plans to dispatch or supervisor
- Ensure communication devices are charged and functional

**Adverse Conditions:**

When conditions deteriorate, drivers shall:

- Reduce speed appropriate to conditions
- Increase following distance (4+ seconds in poor conditions)
- Use headlights in reduced visibility
- Pull over safely if conditions become too dangerous
- Contact supervision for guidance when conditions are severe
- Never proceed if conditions exceed safe operating limits

**Journey Postponement:**

Journeys shall be postponed or cancelled when:

- Severe weather warnings are in effect
- Road closures or significant hazards are reported
- Driver fatigue or illness prevents safe operation
- Vehicle defects cannot be safely remediated
- Supervisor determines conditions are too hazardous

### 5.5 Incident Reporting

All vehicle incidents shall be reported immediately to the supervisor and documented using company incident reporting procedures.

**Immediate Actions:**

1. Ensure safety of all persons involved
2. Call emergency services if injuries require medical attention
3. Move vehicles to safe location if possible and safe to do so
4. Exchange information with other parties as required by law
5. Do not admit fault or liability at the scene
6. Document the scene with photographs if safe to do so
7. Report to supervisor immediately
8. Complete incident report within 24 hours

**Documentation Required:**

- Date, time, and location of incident
- Description of events leading to incident
- Weather and road conditions
- Names and contact information of all parties
- Insurance information of other parties
- Police report number if applicable
- Witness information
- Photographs of damage and scene
- Injuries sustained, if any

### 5.6 Winter Driving

**Vehicle Preparation:**

Winter driving shall only be conducted in vehicles equipped with:

- Winter tires (M+S or mountain snowflake rated) from October 1 to April 30
- Functioning heating and defrost systems
- Adequate windshield washer fluid rated for freezing temperatures
- Emergency kit including blankets, food, water, flashlight, and shovel
- Fully charged communication device
- Fuel tank maintained above half full

**Winter Driving Practices:**

Drivers shall:

- Reduce speed to match road conditions, regardless of posted limits
- Increase following distance to minimum 6 seconds on snow/ice
- Avoid sudden acceleration, braking, or steering inputs
- Use gentle throttle and braking inputs
- Clear all snow and ice from vehicle before departure
- Allow additional time for winter journeys
- Monitor weather conditions throughout the journey

**Black Ice and Freezing Rain:**

When black ice or freezing rain is present or forecasted:

- Avoid travel unless absolutely necessary
- Reduce speed significantly below posted limits
- Use bridges and overpasses with extreme caution
- Pull over safely if conditions become too hazardous

### 5.7 Towing Operations

**Authorization:**

Towing operations require:

- Supervisor approval for each towing operation
- Minimum age of 25 years for the driver
- Completion of towing-specific training
- Valid license for the combined vehicle weight

**Equipment Requirements:**

Towing shall only be conducted with:

- Appropriate trailer hitch rated for the load
- Safety chains properly connected in X-pattern
- Functioning trailer lights and brakes
- Properly adjusted tow mirrors
- Load within trailer weight rating and vehicle towing capacity

**Operating Procedures:**

When towing:

- Conduct enhanced pre-trip inspection of tow vehicle and trailer
- Verify coupler is fully seated and locked
- Check trailer tire pressure and condition
- Allow additional stopping distance (double normal following distance)
- Reduce speed, particularly on curves and grades
- Use lower gears for grades and descents
- Check trailer connections and load securing at each stop
