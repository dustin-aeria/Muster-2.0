## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for first aid services and supplies to ensure prompt treatment of injuries and illnesses at Aeria Solutions worksites.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions worksites and operations
- All personnel, contractors, and visitors
- Field operations, remote locations, and office settings

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 First Aid Requirements

1. Adequate first aid equipment, supplies, and trained personnel shall be provided at all worksites.

2. First aid requirements are based on:
   - Number of workers
   - Distance from hospital
   - Hazards present
   - WorkSafeBC First Aid Assessment

### 4.2 Minimum First Aid Provisions

| Workplace Type | Minimum Kit | First Aider |
|:---------------|:------------|:------------|
| **Office (1-10 workers)** | Basic kit | OFA Level 1 on-site |
| **Field crew (2-5 workers)** | Level 1 kit | OFA Level 1 on crew |
| **Remote operations (>20 min from hospital)** | Level 2 kit | OFA Level 2 recommended |
| **High-hazard operations** | Per assessment | Per assessment |

### 4.3 First Aid Kits

First aid kits shall:
- Meet BC OHS Regulation Schedule 3-A requirements
- Be readily accessible and clearly marked
- Be inspected monthly
- Be restocked after use
- Not contain expired supplies
- Be protected from contamination

**Standard Kit Locations:**
- Each company vehicle
- Each RPAS field kit
- Office/shop location
- As identified on FLHA for remote sites

### 4.4 First Aid Training

| Certification | Who | Renewal |
|:--------------|:----|:--------|
| **OFA Level 1** | All field workers (minimum) | 3 years |
| **OFA Level 2** | Designated for remote operations | 3 years |
| **OFA Level 3** | As required by assessment | 3 years |
| **Basic CPR** | Encouraged for all personnel | 1 year |

At least one person with appropriate first aid certification shall be present during all operations.

### 4.5 First Aid Response

1. **Immediate Response:**
   - Assess scene safety
   - Provide first aid within scope of training
   - Call emergency services if needed
   - Do not move seriously injured persons unless in danger

2. **Documentation:**
   - Record all first aid treatments
   - Complete First Aid Record (FRM-FAR)
   - Report injuries per incident reporting requirements

3. **Follow-up:**
   - Ensure injured worker receives appropriate medical care
   - Monitor worker's condition
   - Investigate incident per HSE-008

### 4.6 Communication for Remote Operations

For operations more than 20 minutes from a hospital:
- Ensure reliable communication method
- Document emergency contact numbers
- Know evacuation routes
- Consider satellite communication for remote areas

### 4.7 Records

First aid records shall include:
- Date and time of injury/illness
- Name of injured worker
- Description of injury/illness
- First aid provided
- Name of first aider
- Follow-up actions

Records shall be retained per ADM-001-PR and kept confidential.
