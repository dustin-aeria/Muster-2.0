## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for protecting the environment during Aeria Solutions operations, including prevention of spills, proper waste management, and minimizing impact on sensitive areas.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions operations
- All personnel, contractors, and subcontractors
- All worksites including remote and wilderness areas

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Environmental Commitment

Aeria Solutions is committed to:
- Minimizing environmental impact of operations
- Complying with all environmental regulations
- Protecting sensitive ecosystems and wildlife
- Preventing pollution and spills
- Proper waste management and disposal

### 4.2 Spill Prevention

1. **Fuel and Hazardous Materials:**
   - Transport fuels in approved containers
   - Use secondary containment when refueling
   - Maintain spill kits at all operations involving fuels or chemicals
   - Inspect containers for leaks before transport

2. **Battery Management:**
   - Store and transport lithium batteries per manufacturer requirements
   - Dispose of damaged batteries properly
   - Never dispose of batteries in regular waste

### 4.3 Spill Response

| Spill Size | Response |
|:-----------|:---------|
| **Minor** (<5L fuel) | Contain; clean up; report to supervisor |
| **Moderate** (5-100L) | Contain; clean up; report to Operations Manager |
| **Major** (>100L or to water) | Contain; evacuate; report to emergency services and agencies |

**Reportable Spills (BC):**
- Any spill to water
- >100L petroleum products on land
- Any hazardous substance per Spill Reporting Regulation

Report to: Provincial Emergency Program (1-800-663-3456)

### 4.4 Waste Management

| Waste Type | Handling |
|:-----------|:---------|
| **General Waste** | Bag and remove from site; dispose properly |
| **Hazardous Waste** | Segregate; label; dispose through approved facilities |
| **Batteries** | Return to depot; do not dispose in regular waste |
| **Electronic Waste** | Return for proper recycling |
| **Used Oil/Fuel** | Contain; return to approved disposal |

**Pack In / Pack Out:** All materials brought to a site must be removed. Leave no trace of operations.

### 4.5 Wildlife Protection

1. **General Requirements:**
   - Do not approach or disturb wildlife
   - Maintain safe distance from wildlife
   - Cease operations if wildlife is affected
   - Report wildlife incidents

2. **Sensitive Species:**
   - Be aware of nesting seasons and locations
   - Follow buffer distances for sensitive species
   - Check for wildlife restrictions before operations

3. **RPAS and Wildlife:**
   - Maintain distance from wildlife per regulations
   - Cease flight if animals show disturbance behavior
   - Follow any wildlife-related SFOC conditions

### 4.6 Protected Areas

Operations in or near protected areas require:
- Prior authorization from relevant authority
- Compliance with specific restrictions
- Enhanced environmental precautions
- Documentation of compliance

Protected areas include: parks, ecological reserves, wildlife management areas, and heritage sites.

### 4.7 Noise and Disturbance

- Minimize noise impact on wildlife and communities
- Consider timing of operations near sensitive areas
- Use appropriate flight altitudes to minimize disturbance
- Communicate with local stakeholders when appropriate

### 4.8 Documentation

Environmental documentation includes:
- Spill reports
- Waste disposal records
- Permits for operations in protected areas
- Environmental incident reports
