## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the rights and responsibilities of all workers regarding health and safety at Aeria Solutions Ltd, in accordance with the BC Workers Compensation Act and BC OHS Regulation.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Aeria Solutions personnel, including:
- Employees
- Contractors and subcontractors
- Trainees and students
- Any person performing work under Aeria direction

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 Worker Rights

Every worker has the following rights:

#### Right to Know
- Be informed of all known or reasonably foreseeable hazards
- Receive information, instruction, and training to work safely
- Access to hazard assessments, SDSs, and safety information
- Know the results of workplace inspections and air quality testing

#### Right to Participate
- Participate in workplace health and safety activities
- Accompany safety inspectors during workplace inspections
- Be consulted on matters affecting health and safety
- Make recommendations to improve safety
- Participate in incident investigations

#### Right to Refuse Unsafe Work
- Refuse to perform work that presents an undue hazard
- Refuse without fear of disciplinary action
- Be informed of the investigation outcome
- See HSE-018 and HSE-012-PR for the refusal procedure

### 5.2 Worker Responsibilities

Every worker shall:

#### General Duties
- Take reasonable care to protect their own health and safety and that of others
- Follow safe work procedures and use required safety equipment
- Not engage in horseplay or conduct that could endanger anyone
- Report to work fit for duty (not impaired by alcohol, drugs, or fatigue)

#### Compliance
- Comply with the BC Workers Compensation Act and OHS Regulation
- Comply with Aeria Solutions safety policies and procedures
- Use personal protective equipment as required
- Operate equipment only if trained and authorized

#### Reporting
- Report hazards, unsafe conditions, or unsafe acts immediately
- Report injuries, incidents, and near-misses immediately
- Report defective equipment before use
- Report any limitations affecting ability to work safely

#### Cooperation
- Cooperate with supervisors, safety personnel, and WorkSafeBC officers
- Participate in safety meetings and training
- Cooperate in incident investigations
- Provide accurate information on safety matters

### 5.3 Supervisor Responsibilities

Supervisors shall:

- Ensure workers are made aware of hazards
- Ensure workers have required training and PPE
- Ensure safe work procedures are followed
- Correct unsafe conditions or acts immediately
- Investigate and report incidents
- Lead by example in safety compliance

### 5.4 Employer Responsibilities

Aeria Solutions Ltd shall:

- Establish and maintain a health and safety program
- Provide a safe workplace, equipment, and systems
- Provide required information, instruction, training, and supervision
- Consult and cooperate with workers on safety matters
- Investigate incidents and implement corrective actions
- Not take discriminatory action against workers exercising safety rights

---

## 6. No Reprisal

Aeria Solutions will not take or threaten any discriminatory action against a worker for:
- Exercising any right under the Workers Compensation Act
- Refusing unsafe work
- Reporting hazards or safety concerns
- Participating in safety activities

Any perceived reprisal should be reported to the Accountable Executive immediately.
