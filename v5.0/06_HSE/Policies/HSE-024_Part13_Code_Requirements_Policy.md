## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish compliance requirements with WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 13 (Ladders, Scaffolds, and Temporary Work Platforms) as applicable to Aeria Solutions Ltd RPAS operations. While RPAS operations are primarily ground-based, certain activities may involve elevated work or temporary structures at field sites, including equipment staging, antenna installation, ground control point placement, and access to observation positions.

This policy ensures that when elevated work or temporary structures are required, they are used safely and in compliance with regulatory requirements, protecting workers from fall hazards and structural failures.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Aeria Solutions Ltd employees and contractors
- All worksites where ladders, scaffolds, or temporary structures are used
- All elevated work activities conducted during RPAS operations

**Activities covered:**

| Activity | Description |
|:---------|:------------|
| **Equipment Staging** | Accessing elevated positions for equipment placement or retrieval |
| **Antenna Installation** | Temporary antenna masts or elevated communication equipment |
| **Ground Control Points** | GCP placement on structures or elevated surfaces |
| **Observation Positions** | Accessing elevated positions for visual observation during flights |
| **Vehicle Access** | Using ladders to access truck beds, trailers, or rooftops |
| **Building Access** | Accessing rooftops or elevated areas at client facilities |
| **Temporary Shelters** | Temporary structures for weather protection or equipment storage |

**Exclusions:**

- Fixed building stairs and permanent infrastructure (covered by facility owner)
- Work at heights requiring fall protection systems beyond ladder use (requires additional planning and fall protection program)
- Construction scaffolding for building purposes (specialized contractor required)

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to preventing injuries from falls and structural failures during RPAS operations. The company recognizes that while RPAS operations minimize the need for elevated work compared to traditional inspection methods, some elevated work activities may still be necessary.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Hierarchy of Controls:** Wherever possible, eliminate the need for elevated work through ground-based alternatives or RPAS capabilities.

2. **Proper Equipment:** When elevated work is necessary, only approved equipment in good condition shall be used.

3. **Competent Workers:** Only trained and competent workers shall perform elevated work or erect temporary structures.

4. **Risk Assessment:** All elevated work shall be planned with appropriate hazard assessment and controls.

5. **Regulatory Compliance:** All activities shall comply with WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Part 13 and related requirements.

6. **Continuous Improvement:** Near-misses and incidents related to elevated work shall be reported and investigated to prevent recurrence.

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Competent Person** | A person who is qualified because of knowledge, training, and experience to organize the work and its performance, is familiar with the Act and regulations, and has knowledge of any potential or actual danger to health or safety in the workplace |
| **Elevated Work** | Any work activity performed at a height where a fall could result in injury, typically defined as 10 feet (3 meters) or more above grade |
| **Extension Ladder** | A non-self-supporting ladder consisting of two or more sections that can be adjusted for overall length |
| **Fall Protection** | Methods used to prevent workers from falling or to arrest a fall, including guardrails, safety nets, and personal fall arrest systems |
| **Fixed Ladder** | A ladder permanently attached to a structure |
| **Portable Ladder** | A ladder designed to be easily moved, including stepladders and extension ladders |
| **Scaffold** | A temporary elevated platform and supporting structure used for supporting workers, materials, or both |
| **Stepladder** | A self-supporting portable ladder, non-adjustable in length, with flat steps and a hinged back |
| **Temporary Structure** | Any structure erected temporarily at a worksite for operational purposes, including antenna masts, shelters, and platforms |
| **Three-Point Contact** | A climbing technique maintaining contact with the ladder using two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand, at all times |

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Ladder Requirements

**General Requirements:**

All ladders used in company operations shall:

- Meet CSA Standard CAN3-Z11-M81 or equivalent
- Be appropriate for the intended task and conditions
- Be inspected before each use
- Be maintained in good condition
- Be used only for purposes for which they are designed
- Bear manufacturer's labels indicating duty rating and proper use

**Ladder Types and Applications:**

| Ladder Type | Application | Maximum Height |
|:------------|:------------|:---------------|
| **Step Ladder** | Vehicle access, low-level equipment access | Top step not used; follow manufacturer limits |
| **Extension Ladder** | Building access, elevated positions | Follow 4:1 ratio; extend 3 feet above landing |
| **Platform Ladder** | Extended work at moderate heights | Platform designed for standing |
| **Articulating Ladder** | Multiple configurations for varied needs | Follow manufacturer specifications |

**Prohibited Ladder Uses:**

- Metal ladders near electrical hazards
- Damaged or defective ladders
- Ladders on unstable or slippery surfaces without proper stabilization
- Standing on top cap or top step of stepladders
- Using ladders in high wind conditions
- Overreaching while on a ladder
- Carrying loads that prevent three-point contact

**Ladder Inspection:**

Before each use, inspect for:

- Cracks, breaks, or corrosion in rails and rungs
- Loose, bent, or missing rungs or steps
- Damaged or missing safety feet
- Damaged or missing hardware and locks
- Defective ropes on extension ladders
- Oil, grease, or other slippery substances
- Manufacturer labels and duty rating visible

**Ladder Setup:**

| Requirement | Specification |
|:------------|:--------------|
| **Surface** | Level, firm, non-slippery base |
| **Angle** | Extension ladders at 4:1 ratio (1 foot out for every 4 feet up) |
| **Extension Above Landing** | Minimum 3 feet (1 meter) above landing surface |
| **Securing** | Tie off at top or secure at base; have worker hold if necessary |
| **Clearance** | Minimum 6 inches clearance behind rungs |
| **Opening Fully** | Stepladders fully opened with spreaders locked |

**Ladder Use:**

Workers using ladders shall:

- Face the ladder when ascending or descending
- Maintain three-point contact at all times
- Keep body centered between side rails
- Not carry tools or materials that prevent three-point contact (use tool belt or hoist line)
- Not overreach; move the ladder instead
- Not use ladder in front of doors unless door secured open or locked
- Descend ladder before moving it

### 5.2 Elevated Work Requirements

**Planning Requirements:**

Before performing elevated work:

- Assess whether elevated work can be eliminated or reduced
- Consider RPAS alternatives for inspection or observation tasks
- Identify fall hazards and required controls
- Ensure workers are trained and competent
- Verify appropriate equipment is available and in good condition
- Check weather conditions; postpone if high winds or ice present

**Fall Protection Thresholds:**

| Situation | Fall Protection Required |
|:----------|:------------------------|
| **3 meters (10 feet) or more** | Fall protection required by regulation |
| **Any unguarded edge** | Guardrails or fall restraint regardless of height |
| **Roof work** | Fall protection if within 2 meters of edge |
| **Ladder use** | Three-point contact; fall protection if hands needed for work |

**Fall Protection Methods:**

When fall protection is required, use in order of preference:

1. **Guardrails** - Permanent or temporary barriers at unguarded edges
2. **Fall Restraint** - System preventing worker from reaching fall hazard
3. **Fall Arrest** - Personal fall arrest system to arrest a fall in progress

**Personal Fall Arrest Systems:**

If personal fall arrest systems are used:

- Full body harness required (not body belts)
- System components compatible and rated
- Adequate clearance to prevent striking lower level
- Secure anchor point rated for 5000 lbs per worker
- Daily inspection before use
- Training in system use and rescue procedures

**Client Facility Requirements:**

When elevated work is required at client facilities:

- Comply with client work permit systems
- Use client-designated access points and equipment if required
- Coordinate with client safety representative
- Follow more stringent of company or client requirements

### 5.3 Temporary Structure Requirements

**Scope of Temporary Structures:**

Temporary structures used in RPAS operations may include:

| Structure Type | Application |
|:---------------|:------------|
| **Antenna Masts** | Temporary communication or data link antenna supports |
| **Equipment Shelters** | Pop-up tents or temporary shelters for equipment protection |
| **Observation Platforms** | Temporary raised platforms for visual observers |
| **Shade Structures** | Canopies for crew comfort in extreme weather |
| **Tripods/Monopods** | Support structures for ground control equipment |

**General Requirements:**

All temporary structures shall:

- Be designed and constructed to support intended loads with safety factor
- Be stable against wind, weather, and foreseeable forces
- Be erected by competent persons following manufacturer instructions
- Be inspected before use and regularly during use
- Be maintained in safe condition
- Be dismantled in reverse order of erection

**Antenna Mast Requirements:**

Temporary antenna masts shall:

- Be rated for the equipment weight and wind conditions
- Be properly guyed or secured to prevent tipping
- Be positioned away from overhead power lines (minimum 3 meters)
- Have guy wires clearly marked with flagging
- Be lowered in high wind conditions exceeding manufacturer limits
- Be grounded for lightning protection if required by conditions

**Scaffold Use (Limited Application):**

If scaffolds are required (uncommon for RPAS operations):

- Scaffolds shall be erected by competent persons
- Follow WorkSafeBC Part 13 requirements for specific scaffold type
- Platform fully planked with guardrails
- Access ladders secured to scaffold
- Load limits posted and not exceeded
- Daily inspection by competent person before use

**Tent and Shelter Structures:**

Pop-up tents and temporary shelters shall:

- Be properly staked or weighted to prevent wind displacement
- Not be used in wind conditions exceeding manufacturer limits
- Be positioned away from traffic areas and hazards
- Have adequate clearance for safe entry and exit
- Be taken down in deteriorating weather conditions

### 5.4 Inspection and Maintenance

**Inspection Requirements:**

| Equipment | Inspection Frequency | By Whom |
|:----------|:--------------------|:--------|
| **Portable Ladders** | Before each use | User |
| **Portable Ladders** | Annually (documented) | Competent person |
| **Extension Ladders** | Before each use | User |
| **Fall Protection Equipment** | Before each use and annually | Competent person |
| **Temporary Structures** | Before use and after any modification | Competent person |

**Inspection Documentation:**

- Pre-use inspections may be informal but defects must be reported
- Annual inspections documented on inspection forms
- Defective equipment tagged out of service immediately
- Inspection records retained for 3 years

**Maintenance:**

- Ladders cleaned and stored properly after use
- Damaged equipment repaired by qualified persons or replaced
- No field modifications to ladders or fall protection equipment
- Manufacturer maintenance recommendations followed

**Out of Service Equipment:**

Equipment found defective shall be:

- Immediately removed from service
- Tagged "DO NOT USE" or similar marking
- Reported to supervisor
- Repaired by qualified person or disposed of properly
- Not returned to service until inspected by competent person

### 5.5 Training Requirements

**Ladder Safety Training:**

All workers who use ladders shall receive training on:

- Ladder types and selection for tasks
- Inspection procedures and defect recognition
- Proper setup and angle
- Safe climbing techniques (three-point contact)
- Prohibited uses and common hazards
- Fall protection requirements when applicable

**Elevated Work Training:**

Workers performing elevated work at height shall receive:

- Fall hazard recognition
- Fall protection equipment use and inspection
- Rescue procedures if fall arrest systems used
- Site-specific hazards and procedures

**Temporary Structure Training:**

Workers erecting or using temporary structures shall receive:

- Structure-specific erection and dismantling procedures
- Load limits and capacity
- Stability requirements
- Inspection procedures
- Weather limitations

**Training Documentation:**

- Training records maintained for all workers
- Refresher training when procedures change or incidents occur
- Competency verified before workers perform elevated work independently
