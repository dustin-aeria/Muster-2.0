## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for protecting workers from heat and cold stress hazards during outdoor operations. Working in extreme temperatures can cause serious illness or injury.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions field operations
- All personnel working outdoors
- Operations in all seasons and climates

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Heat Stress** | The body's inability to cool itself adequately in hot conditions |
| **Cold Stress** | The body's inability to maintain core temperature in cold conditions |
| **Humidex** | Combined temperature and humidity index |
| **Wind Chill** | Combined temperature and wind index |
| **Acclimatization** | Gradual adaptation to hot or cold conditions |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 General Requirements

1. Temperature-related hazards shall be assessed before outdoor work
2. Controls shall be implemented to protect workers
3. Workers shall be trained to recognize symptoms
4. Adequate facilities, equipment, and fluids shall be provided

---

## PART A: HEAT STRESS

### 5.2 Heat Risk Assessment

Consider heat stress risk when:
- Temperature exceeds 25°C
- Humidex exceeds 30
- Work involves physical exertion
- Workers wear PPE or protective clothing
- Workers are not acclimatized

### 5.3 Heat Risk Levels

| Humidex | Risk Level | Action |
|:--------|:-----------|:-------|
| 30-34 | Low | Awareness; hydration |
| 35-39 | Moderate | Rest breaks; shade; monitor |
| 40-44 | High | Modified schedule; frequent breaks; close monitoring |
| 45+ | Extreme | Consider postponing; emergency measures |

### 5.4 Heat Stress Controls

| Control | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Scheduling** | Work during cooler hours; reduce intensity |
| **Hydration** | Water readily available; encourage regular drinking |
| **Rest Breaks** | Frequent breaks in shade or cool area |
| **PPE** | Light, breathable clothing; cooling vests if available |
| **Acclimatization** | Gradual exposure over 1-2 weeks |
| **Buddy System** | Workers monitor each other |

### 5.5 Heat-Related Illness Signs

| Condition | Signs | Response |
|:----------|:------|:---------|
| **Heat Cramps** | Muscle cramps, sweating | Rest; drink fluids; stretch |
| **Heat Exhaustion** | Heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache | Move to cool area; drink fluids; cool skin; monitor |
| **Heat Stroke** | Hot dry skin, confusion, unconsciousness | EMERGENCY: Call 911; cool rapidly; do not give fluids if unconscious |

---

## PART B: COLD STRESS

### 5.6 Cold Risk Assessment

Consider cold stress risk when:
- Temperature below 0°C
- Wind chill below -10°C
- Wet conditions present
- Extended outdoor work
- Workers in contact with cold surfaces

### 5.7 Cold Risk Levels

| Wind Chill | Risk Level | Action |
|:-----------|:-----------|:-------|
| 0 to -9 | Low | Awareness; proper clothing |
| -10 to -27 | Moderate | Warm breaks; limit exposure; monitor |
| -28 to -39 | High | Reduce exposure time; warming shelter; close monitoring |
| -40 and below | Extreme | Limit outdoor work; emergency measures |

### 5.8 Cold Stress Controls

| Control | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Clothing** | Layered, insulated, moisture-wicking; protect extremities |
| **Warming Breaks** | Access to heated shelter or vehicle |
| **Limit Exposure** | Rotate workers; reduce outdoor time |
| **Dry Clothing** | Change wet clothing immediately |
| **Hot Beverages** | Warm (not hot) drinks available |
| **Buddy System** | Workers monitor each other |

### 5.9 Cold-Related Illness Signs

| Condition | Signs | Response |
|:----------|:------|:---------|
| **Frostnip** | Numbness, pale skin | Warm area gradually; do not rub |
| **Frostbite** | Hard, waxy skin; numbness | Move to warm area; immerse in warm water; seek medical care |
| **Hypothermia** | Shivering, confusion, slurred speech | EMERGENCY: Call 911; move to warm area; remove wet clothing; warm core first |

## 6. Training

Workers shall be trained on:
- Recognition of heat and cold stress risks
- Signs and symptoms of heat/cold illness
- Prevention measures
- Response procedures
- When to stop work

### 6.1 Pre-Job Assessment

The FLHA shall include assessment of:
- Current and forecast temperatures
- Humidex or wind chill
- Availability of shade, shelter, water
- Work intensity and duration
- Worker acclimatization and fitness
