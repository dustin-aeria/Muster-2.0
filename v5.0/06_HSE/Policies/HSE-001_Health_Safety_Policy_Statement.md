## Health & Safety Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all workers, contractors, and visitors. We believe that all injuries and occupational illnesses are preventable, and we are dedicated to achieving and maintaining a workplace free from harm.

### Our Commitments

**We will:**

1. **Comply with all applicable laws and regulations**, including the BC Workers Compensation Act, BC OHS Regulation, Canadian Aviation Regulations, and all other relevant health and safety requirements.

2. **Provide a safe work environment** by identifying hazards, assessing risks, and implementing effective controls before work begins.

3. **Provide safe equipment and systems** that are properly maintained and fit for purpose.

4. **Provide information, instruction, training, and supervision** necessary for all workers to perform their jobs safely.

5. **Consult and communicate** with workers on health and safety matters and encourage their active participation in making the workplace safe.

6. **Investigate all incidents** to determine root causes and implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence.

7. **Allocate adequate resources** — financial, human, and material — to implement and maintain our health and safety program.

8. **Continuously improve** our health and safety performance through regular review, measurement, and action.

### Worker Rights

All workers have the right to:
- Know about hazards in the workplace
- Participate in health and safety activities
- Refuse unsafe work without fear of reprisal

### Shared Responsibility

Health and safety is everyone's responsibility:
- **Management** is responsible for providing safe work conditions and leading by example
- **Supervisors** are responsible for ensuring safe work practices are followed
- **Workers** are responsible for following safe work practices and reporting hazards

### Stop Work Authority

**Every person has the authority and responsibility to stop any work they believe is unsafe.** No job is so important that we cannot take time to do it safely.
