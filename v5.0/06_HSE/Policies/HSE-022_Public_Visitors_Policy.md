## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish requirements for managing the safety of members of the public and visitors who may be present at or near Aeria Solutions Ltd worksites. RPAS operations frequently occur in public areas, near occupied properties, or at client sites where the public may be present. This policy ensures appropriate measures are implemented to protect both the public and authorized visitors from operational hazards.

This policy supports compliance with Transport Canada regulations requiring RPAS operations to maintain safe distances from bystanders and implements WorkSafeBC requirements for visitor safety on worksites.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Aeria Solutions Ltd employees and contractors
- All RPAS operations conducted in public or semi-public areas
- All worksites where members of the public may be present
- All visitors to company facilities or field operations
- Client representatives observing operations

**Categories of persons covered:**

| Category | Definition |
|:---------|:-----------|
| **Members of the Public** | Persons not associated with the operation who may be present in the area, including pedestrians, property owners, and passersby |
| **Authorized Visitors** | Persons invited to observe operations, including client representatives, stakeholders, and media |
| **Incidental Visitors** | Persons who approach the worksite out of curiosity or to make inquiries |
| **Third-Party Workers** | Other contractors or workers present at shared worksites |

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to protecting members of the public and visitors from hazards associated with RPAS operations. The company recognizes that our operations occur in diverse environments where interaction with the public is common and must be managed safely.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Public Safety Priority:** Operations shall not proceed if they pose unacceptable risks to members of the public.

2. **Regulatory Compliance:** All operations shall maintain required distances from non-participants as specified by Transport Canada regulations.

3. **Effective Communication:** The public shall be informed of operations and associated hazards through appropriate signage, barriers, and direct communication.

4. **Visitor Protection:** All visitors to worksites shall receive appropriate safety briefings and supervision.

5. **Incident Response:** Any incident involving the public or visitors shall be immediately reported and investigated.

6. **Professional Interaction:** All interactions with the public shall be conducted professionally and courteously.

---

## 4. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Bystander** | Any person not directly involved in or essential to the RPAS operation who is present in the operational area |
| **Controlled Access Area** | A designated area where access is restricted and controlled through physical barriers, signage, or personnel |
| **Ground Crew** | Personnel directly involved in RPAS operations, including pilots, visual observers, and support staff |
| **Hazard Zone** | The area around RPAS operations where there is potential risk from the aircraft, including the flight area and emergency landing zones |
| **Incursion** | Entry of an unauthorized person into a controlled access area or hazard zone |
| **Non-Participant** | Any person not directly involved in the RPAS operation, including members of the public and uninvolved third parties |
| **Safety Briefing** | A verbal or written communication to visitors outlining site hazards, safety rules, and emergency procedures |
| **Site Control** | Measures implemented to manage access to and movement within a worksite |
| **Visitor** | Any person who is not a regular employee or contractor of Aeria Solutions Ltd but who has been authorized to be present at a worksite |

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Site Control Measures

**Pre-Operation Planning:**

Before commencing operations where public interaction is possible:

- Conduct site assessment to identify public access points and traffic patterns
- Determine appropriate site control measures based on location and hazards
- Coordinate with property owners, event organizers, or local authorities as required
- Plan visual observer positioning to monitor for public approach
- Identify safe areas for interested observers if appropriate

**Physical Controls:**

Site control may include:

| Control Measure | Application |
|:----------------|:------------|
| **Signage** | Warning signs at access points indicating RPAS operations in progress |
| **Barrier Tape** | Visible tape or flagging to delineate controlled areas |
| **Pylons/Cones** | Traffic control devices to redirect pedestrian or vehicle traffic |
| **Fencing** | Temporary fencing for high-traffic areas or extended operations |
| **Personnel** | Ground crew positioned to intercept and redirect approaching persons |

**Minimum Distance Requirements:**

Operations shall maintain Transport Canada required distances:

- Standard operations: 30 meters horizontal distance from non-participants
- Operations under SFOC or exemption: as specified in the authorization
- Operations in controlled or public areas: enhanced site control measures required

**Public Areas:**

When operating in public areas (parks, streets, public lands):

- Additional ground personnel may be required for site control
- Operations may need to pause when maintaining safe distances is not possible
- Consider timing operations for periods of lower public traffic
- Coordinate with local authorities for high-visibility operations

### 5.2 Safety Briefings

**Visitor Briefings:**

All authorized visitors shall receive a safety briefing before entering the work area, covering:

- Nature of operations and associated hazards
- Boundaries of the safe observation area
- Required personal protective equipment
- Emergency procedures and assembly points
- Rules for the visit (no touching equipment, stay with escort, etc.)
- Communication protocols and who to contact with concerns
- Expected duration of visit

**Briefing Documentation:**

- Visitors shall sign acknowledgment of receiving the safety briefing
- Briefing records shall be maintained with project documentation
- Visitor sign-in logs shall record arrival and departure times

**Briefing Delivery:**

- Briefings shall be delivered by competent personnel
- Language and content appropriate to the audience
- Questions encouraged and answered before proceeding
- Written briefing materials provided for complex operations

### 5.3 Supervision Requirements

**Escort Requirements:**

Visitors shall be supervised at all times while in operational areas:

| Visitor Category | Supervision Requirement |
|:-----------------|:-----------------------|
| **Client Representatives** | Escorted by project lead or designated personnel |
| **Stakeholders/Officials** | Escorted by supervisor or management |
| **Media** | Escorted by designated company representative |
| **Incidental Visitors** | Redirected to safe area or escorted off site |
| **Third-Party Workers** | Coordinated supervision with their employer |

**Escort Responsibilities:**

The escort shall:

- Remain with the visitor at all times
- Ensure visitor compliance with safety rules
- Position visitor in safe observation areas
- Intervene if visitor enters hazard zones
- Respond to visitor questions and concerns
- Facilitate visitor departure when appropriate

**Visitor Behavior:**

Visitors shall:

- Remain in designated observation areas
- Follow all instructions from company personnel
- Not touch, handle, or interfere with equipment
- Not enter flight areas or hazard zones
- Not use personal RPAS or interfere with operations
- Report any safety concerns immediately

### 5.4 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

**Visitor PPE Requirements:**

Visitors shall be provided with and required to wear appropriate PPE based on site hazards:

| Site Condition | Required PPE |
|:---------------|:-------------|
| **Standard field operations** | High-visibility vest, closed-toe footwear |
| **Construction sites** | Hard hat, high-visibility vest, safety footwear, safety glasses |
| **Industrial facilities** | As required by facility operator plus high-visibility vest |
| **Remote/rugged terrain** | Appropriate footwear, weather protection |
| **Near water** | PFD if within 2 meters of water edge |

**PPE Provision:**

- Company shall provide visitor PPE (vests, hard hats) where required
- Visitors shall be advised in advance of footwear and clothing requirements
- Visitors without appropriate PPE shall not be permitted in operational areas
- PPE shall be in good condition and properly sized

**Client Site Requirements:**

When operating at client facilities:

- Follow client PPE requirements, which may exceed company standards
- Ensure visitors comply with client site rules
- Coordinate visitor access with client site management

### 5.5 Liability and Insurance

**Public Liability:**

Aeria Solutions Ltd maintains comprehensive general liability insurance that includes:

- Coverage for bodily injury to third parties
- Coverage for property damage
- RPAS-specific aviation liability coverage
- Completed operations coverage

**Visitor Waivers:**

For authorized visitors:

- Visitors may be required to sign acknowledgment of risks and release
- Waivers do not eliminate duty of care but document informed consent
- Waivers shall be reviewed by legal counsel
- Signed waivers retained with project records

**Incident Documentation:**

Any incident involving the public or visitors shall be:

- Documented immediately with detailed incident report
- Photographed with consent where appropriate
- Reported to management immediately
- Reported to insurance carrier within 24 hours if injury or damage occurred
- Retained for minimum 7 years

**Third-Party Property:**

Operations shall not damage third-party property:

- Maintain safe distances from structures, vehicles, and property
- Obtain permission before overflying private property
- Document any pre-existing conditions at sensitive sites
- Report any property damage immediately

### 5.6 Public Interaction Protocols

**Approaching Members of the Public:**

When members of the public approach the operation:

1. Visual observer alerts pilot-in-command
2. Pilot assesses need to pause or relocate operations
3. Ground crew member approaches person professionally
4. Explain nature of operations briefly
5. Direct person to safe observation area or request they maintain distance
6. Resume operations when safe to do so

**Responding to Inquiries:**

Personnel shall respond to public inquiries:

- Professionally and courteously at all times
- Provide general information about operations if appropriate
- Refer media inquiries to management
- Not disclose client information without authorization
- Provide company contact information for follow-up concerns

**Managing Difficult Situations:**

If a member of the public is uncooperative or aggressive:

- Remain calm and professional
- Do not argue or escalate the situation
- Pause operations if safety is compromised
- Contact supervisor for guidance
- Contact local authorities if person refuses to leave or becomes threatening
- Document the incident thoroughly

**Photography and Recording:**

Personnel and visitors may take photographs of operations subject to:

- Client approval for client site operations
- No capture of proprietary equipment details
- No posting to social media without company approval
- Respect for privacy of other persons present
