## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish comprehensive security requirements for Aeria Solutions Ltd to protect personnel, physical assets, information, and data from unauthorized access, theft, damage, or misuse. As an RPAS service provider, the company handles sensitive client information, valuable equipment, and proprietary data that require protection through robust security measures.

This policy provides the framework for physical security at worksites and facilities, equipment security during transport and storage, information security for digital and physical data, and incident response procedures for security breaches.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Aeria Solutions Ltd employees and contractors
- All company facilities, vehicles, and field worksites
- All company-owned equipment, including RPAS systems and accessories
- All information systems, databases, and software platforms
- All client data and project information in any format
- All visitors to company premises or worksites

**Security domains covered:**

- Physical security (facilities, worksites, equipment)
- Personnel security (access control, identification)
- Information security (data protection, cybersecurity)
- Operational security (RPAS equipment, flight data)
- Transportation security (equipment in transit)

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Aeria Solutions Ltd is committed to maintaining a secure environment that protects personnel, assets, information, and client interests. Security is a shared responsibility of all personnel, and the company implements a layered security approach proportionate to identified risks.

**Core Principles:**

1. **Protection of People First:** The safety and security of personnel takes precedence over protection of assets or information.

2. **Defense in Depth:** Multiple layers of security controls are implemented to protect critical assets and information.

3. **Need-to-Know Basis:** Access to information and facilities is restricted to those who require it for their duties.

4. **Client Confidentiality:** Client information and project data are protected with the same diligence as company proprietary information.

5. **Incident Response:** All security incidents are reported immediately and investigated thoroughly.

6. **Continuous Improvement:** Security measures are regularly reviewed and updated based on risk assessments and incident lessons learned.

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Site Access Control

**Facility Security:**

Company facilities shall be protected through:

- Locked entry points with key or access card control
- Visitor sign-in procedures with escort requirements
- Security lighting on building exteriors and parking areas
- Secure storage areas for equipment and sensitive materials
- Clear desk policy for workstations
- Secure disposal of confidential documents

**Field Worksite Security:**

Field operations shall implement appropriate security measures including:

- Site perimeter establishment with visual markers where appropriate
- Vehicle parking positioning to deter unauthorized access
- Equipment staging in observable locations
- Continuous visual monitoring of equipment during operations
- Secure storage of equipment in vehicles when not in use
- Coordination with client site security where applicable

**Access Authorization:**

- Access to company facilities requires management authorization
- Access levels are assigned based on job requirements
- Access authorizations are reviewed annually
- Access is immediately revoked upon termination or role change
- Temporary access for contractors requires supervisor approval
- Master keys and access cards are inventoried and tracked

### 5.2 Equipment Security

**RPAS Equipment Protection:**

RPAS systems and related equipment shall be:

- Inventoried with serial numbers and identifying information
- Stored in secure, locked locations when not in use
- Transported in locked vehicles with equipment out of sight
- Never left unattended in public or unsecured areas
- Tracked through check-out/check-in procedures
- Reported immediately if lost, stolen, or damaged

**Vehicle Security:**

Company vehicles containing equipment shall:

- Be locked at all times when unattended
- Have windows closed when containing equipment
- Park in well-lit, observable locations
- Use canopy locks or secured cargo areas
- Not display company identification that attracts theft
- Be alarmed or use anti-theft devices where practical

**Equipment Transport:**

During transport, equipment security is maintained by:

- Using locked cases and containers
- Keeping equipment out of sight in vehicles
- Not leaving equipment in vehicles overnight
- Securing equipment during fuel stops and breaks
- Maintaining visual contact during loading/unloading

### 5.3 Data Protection

**Data Classification:**

Information is classified according to sensitivity:

| Classification | Description | Examples |
|:---------------|:------------|:---------|
| **Public** | Information approved for public release | Marketing materials, public website content |
| **Internal** | Information for internal use only | Policies, procedures, general communications |
| **Confidential** | Information requiring protection | Client data, project information, financial records |
| **Restricted** | Highly sensitive information with strict access | Personal information, credentials, security codes |

**Data Handling Requirements:**

- Access restricted based on classification and need-to-know
- Encryption required for confidential and restricted data in transit
- Secure storage for confidential and restricted data at rest
- Clean desk policy for physical documents
- Secure disposal through shredding or secure deletion
- No transfer to personal devices without authorization

**Digital Security:**

- Strong, unique passwords for all accounts (minimum 12 characters)
- Multi-factor authentication for sensitive systems
- Regular software updates and security patches
- Antivirus and malware protection on all devices
- Encrypted storage for RPAS data and imagery
- Secure backup procedures with offsite storage
- VPN use for remote access to company systems

**Flight Data Security:**

RPAS flight data and imagery shall be:

- Stored on encrypted drives or secure cloud storage
- Accessible only to authorized project personnel
- Transferred to clients through secure methods
- Retained according to project requirements and contracts
- Securely deleted when retention periods expire

### 5.4 Visitor Management

**Pre-Authorized Visitors:**

Visitors to company facilities shall:

- Be pre-authorized by a company employee
- Sign in at reception, recording name, company, and purpose
- Wear visitor identification while on premises
- Be escorted by an employee at all times
- Be signed out upon departure
- Not be given access to restricted areas without specific authorization

**Field Site Visitors:**

Visitors to field operations shall:

- Be expected and approved by the site supervisor
- Receive a safety briefing before entering the work area
- Be supervised by company personnel at all times
- Comply with all PPE and safety requirements
- Not interfere with operations or handle equipment
- Be logged in site documentation

**Client Representatives:**

Client site visits receive additional accommodations:

- Pre-arranged schedules coordinated with clients
- Safety briefing appropriate to activities observed
- Access to project-specific information as appropriate
- Confidentiality regarding other client projects

### 5.5 Security Incident Response

**Incident Categories:**

| Category | Examples |
|:---------|:---------|
| **Theft** | Equipment theft, vehicle break-in, material loss |
| **Break and Enter** | Unauthorized facility entry, forced entry |
| **Vandalism** | Property damage, equipment sabotage |
| **Trespass** | Unauthorized persons on worksites |
| **Data Breach** | Unauthorized data access, lost devices, hacking |
| **Suspicious Activity** | Surveillance, unusual inquiries, threats |

**Immediate Response:**

Upon discovering a security incident:

1. Ensure personal safety first
2. Do not confront intruders or suspects
3. Call 911 if there is immediate danger or crime in progress
4. Secure the area if safe to do so
5. Notify supervisor immediately
6. Do not disturb evidence
7. Document observations while fresh

**Reporting Requirements:**

All security incidents shall be reported:

- Immediately to supervisor by phone or in person
- Formal written report within 24 hours
- Police report filed for theft, break-in, or criminal activity
- Insurance company notified for loss or damage claims
- Clients notified if their data or project is affected

**Investigation:**

Security incidents will be investigated to:

- Determine root cause and contributing factors
- Identify corrective actions to prevent recurrence
- Assess whether policy or procedure changes are needed
- Document lessons learned
- Support insurance claims or legal proceedings

**Data Breach Response:**

In the event of a data breach:

- Immediately contain the breach if possible
- Notify the Accountable Executive immediately
- Assess the scope and nature of compromised data
- Notify affected individuals as required by law
- Report to Privacy Commissioner if required
- Document all response actions
