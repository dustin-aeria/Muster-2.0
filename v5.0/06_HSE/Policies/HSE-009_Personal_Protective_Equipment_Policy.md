## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for the selection, provision, use, and maintenance of personal protective equipment (PPE) to protect workers from workplace hazards.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions personnel
- Contractors and visitors to worksites
- All operations where PPE is required

## 4. Policy

### 4.1 Hierarchy of Controls

PPE is the **last line of defense**, not the first choice.

Before requiring PPE:
1. Attempt to eliminate the hazard
2. Consider substitution with less hazardous alternatives
3. Apply engineering controls
4. Implement administrative controls

PPE shall be used when hazards cannot be adequately controlled through other means.

### 4.2 Employer Responsibilities

Aeria Solutions shall:
- Identify PPE requirements through hazard assessment
- Provide required PPE at no cost to workers
- Ensure PPE meets applicable standards (CSA, ANSI)
- Train workers on proper use and care
- Maintain and replace PPE as needed
- Enforce PPE requirements

### 4.3 Worker Responsibilities

Workers shall:
- Wear required PPE properly
- Inspect PPE before each use
- Maintain PPE in good condition
- Report defective or damaged PPE immediately
- Not modify or alter PPE
- Store PPE properly when not in use

### 4.4 Minimum PPE Requirements

| Environment | Minimum PPE |
|:------------|:------------|
| **All Field Operations** | Safety footwear, hi-vis vest/clothing |
| **Construction/Industrial Sites** | Hard hat, safety glasses, safety footwear, hi-vis, hearing protection (as required) |
| **Remote/Bush Operations** | As above plus: long sleeves, bear spray (where applicable) |
| **Working Near Water** | PFD/life jacket, appropriate footwear |
| **Hot Work** | Fire-resistant clothing, face shield, gloves |

### 4.5 Task-Specific PPE

Task-specific PPE requirements are documented in:
- Formal Hazard Assessments (FHAs)
- Safe Work Procedures
- Field Level Hazard Assessments (FLHAs)
- Client site requirements

### 4.6 PPE Standards

PPE shall meet applicable standards:

| PPE Type | Standard |
|:---------|:---------|
| Head protection | CSA Z94.1 |
| Eye/face protection | CSA Z94.3 |
| Hearing protection | CSA Z94.2 |
| Safety footwear | CSA Z195 |
| High-visibility apparel | CSA Z96 |
| Fall protection | CSA Z259 series |
| Respiratory protection | CSA Z94.4 |
| Hand protection | Selected for specific hazards |

### 4.7 Inspection and Maintenance

- PPE shall be inspected before each use
- Defective PPE shall be removed from service immediately
- PPE shall be cleaned and maintained per manufacturer instructions
- Damaged PPE shall be replaced promptly
- Fall protection equipment requires formal inspection and documentation

### 4.8 Training

Workers shall receive training on:
- When PPE is required
- What PPE is required
- How to properly don, wear, and doff PPE
- How to inspect PPE
- Limitations of PPE
- Proper storage and care
- How to report defects

### 4.9 Visitors and Contractors

- Visitors must wear minimum site PPE
- Contractors are responsible for providing their own PPE meeting Aeria requirements
- Visitor PPE may be provided for short visits
