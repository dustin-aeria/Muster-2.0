## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements to ensure all workers are fit for duty and not impaired when performing safety-sensitive work. Impairment from any source—including substances, fatigue, illness, or stress—can compromise safety.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions personnel
- Contractors and subcontractors
- Any person performing work under Aeria direction
- All operations, with emphasis on safety-sensitive duties

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Fit for Duty** | Capable of safely performing job duties without impairment |
| **Impairment** | Reduced ability to safely perform duties due to any cause |
| **Safety-Sensitive** | Duties where impairment poses risk to safety |
| **Substances** | Alcohol, cannabis, illicit drugs, and medications that impair |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 Fit for Duty Requirement

1. **All workers must report for duty fit to work safely.**

2. No person shall perform safety-sensitive duties while impaired by:
   - Alcohol
   - Cannabis
   - Illicit drugs
   - Prescription or over-the-counter medications that cause impairment
   - Fatigue
   - Illness or injury
   - Emotional distress or stress

### 5.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following are prohibited during work hours and on company business:

| Prohibited | Applies To |
|:-----------|:-----------|
| Consumption of alcohol | All personnel on duty |
| Consumption of cannabis | All personnel on duty |
| Use of illicit drugs | All personnel at all times |
| Possessing alcohol/cannabis/drugs at worksites | All personnel |
| Reporting for duty impaired | All personnel |

### 5.3 Pre-Duty Requirements

| Substance | Minimum Time Before Duty |
|:----------|:------------------------|
| **Alcohol** | 8 hours ("bottle to throttle") |
| **Cannabis (smoked/vaped)** | 24 hours for safety-sensitive duties |
| **Cannabis (ingested)** | 28 hours for safety-sensitive duties |
| **Impairing medications** | As prescribed; disclose to supervisor |

### 5.4 Medications

Workers taking prescription or over-the-counter medications that may cause impairment shall:
1. Inform their supervisor (general disclosure; medical details not required)
2. Provide documentation that work is safe if requested
3. Not perform safety-sensitive duties if impairment is possible
4. Follow medical guidance regarding work restrictions

### 5.5 Supervisor Responsibilities

Supervisors shall:
- Not permit visibly impaired persons to work
- Address fitness concerns with workers privately
- Arrange safe transportation home if needed
- Document observations and actions
- Report to Operations Manager

### 5.6 Worker Responsibilities

Workers shall:
- Report for duty fit to work
- Disclose impairment concerns to supervisor
- Not perform safety-sensitive duties if impaired
- Report observed impairment in coworkers
- Seek help for substance use issues

### 5.7 Signs of Impairment

| Category | Signs |
|:---------|:------|
| **Physical** | Bloodshot eyes, slurred speech, unsteady movement, odor of alcohol/cannabis |
| **Behavioral** | Confusion, poor judgment, mood swings, unusual behavior |
| **Performance** | Errors, slow reactions, inability to focus, difficulty with routine tasks |

### 5.8 Response to Suspected Impairment

1. Remove worker from safety-sensitive duties
2. Speak privately with the worker
3. Do not accuse; express concern
4. Arrange safe transportation home
5. Document observations objectively
6. Report to Operations Manager

### 5.9 Support and Accommodation

- Workers who seek help for substance use will be supported, not punished
- Reasonable accommodation will be provided per Human Rights Code
- Confidentiality will be maintained
- Return-to-work plans will be developed as appropriate

### 5.10 Consequences

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Removal from duties
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Denial of site access (for contractors)
