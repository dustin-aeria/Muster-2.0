## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for reporting and investigating incidents to identify root causes, prevent recurrence, and meet regulatory requirements. This policy works in conjunction with SMS-005 (Occurrence Reporting) for aviation-specific incidents.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All Aeria Solutions operations and worksites
- All incidents, injuries, and near-misses
- All personnel, contractors, and subcontractors

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|:-----|:-----------|
| **Incident** | An unplanned event that caused or could have caused harm |
| **Near-Miss** | An event that could have resulted in injury or damage but did not |
| **Lost-Time Injury** | An injury requiring time away from work beyond the day of injury |
| **Medical Aid** | An injury requiring treatment beyond first aid |
| **Root Cause** | The fundamental reason an incident occurred |

## 5. Policy

### 5.1 Reporting Requirements

**All incidents and near-misses shall be reported immediately.**

| Incident Type | Report To | Timeframe |
|:--------------|:----------|:----------|
| Any injury | Supervisor/PIC | Immediately |
| Near-miss | Supervisor/PIC | Immediately |
| Equipment damage | Supervisor/PIC | Immediately |
| Serious injury | WorkSafeBC | Immediately (phone) |
| Aviation occurrence | Transport Canada | Per CARs 901.50-901.52 |

### 5.2 Immediate Response

Upon any incident:
1. Ensure safety of all personnel
2. Provide first aid/medical attention as needed
3. Secure the scene (do not disturb evidence)
4. Notify supervisor/PIC immediately
5. Notify emergency services if required
6. Notify Operations Manager

### 5.3 Investigation Requirements

| Incident Type | Investigation Level | Lead |
|:--------------|:-------------------|:-----|
| **Fatality / Serious Injury** | Full formal investigation | External investigator + WorkSafeBC |
| **Lost-Time / Medical Aid** | Formal investigation | Operations Manager |
| **First Aid / Minor** | Basic investigation | PIC/Supervisor |
| **Near-Miss (High Potential)** | Formal investigation | Operations Manager |
| **Near-Miss (Low Potential)** | Basic investigation | PIC/Supervisor |
| **Property Damage (>$500)** | Formal investigation | Operations Manager |

### 5.4 Investigation Process

Investigations shall:

1. **Gather Facts**
   - Interview witnesses (separately)
   - Document the scene (photos, measurements)
   - Review relevant documents (FLHAs, procedures, training records)
   - Preserve physical evidence

2. **Analyze Causes**
   - Identify immediate causes
   - Determine root causes
   - Use systematic methods (5 Whys, fishbone diagram)
   - Consider human factors, equipment, environment, and systems

3. **Develop Corrective Actions**
   - Address root causes, not just symptoms
   - Use hierarchy of controls
   - Assign responsibility and deadlines
   - Verify implementation

4. **Document and Report**
   - Complete investigation report (FRM-INCINV)
   - Share lessons learned
   - Update hazard assessments if needed
   - File required regulatory reports

### 5.5 Documentation

Investigation reports shall include:
- Description of incident
- Date, time, location
- Persons involved
- Injuries and damage
- Facts gathered
- Root cause analysis
- Corrective actions
- Implementation verification

### 5.6 Confidentiality

- Investigation information is confidential
- Focus is on learning, not blame
- Disciplinary action only for willful violations or gross negligence
- Anonymous reporting available through SMS reporting system

### 5.7 External Reporting

| Report To | When Required |
|:----------|:--------------|
| **WorkSafeBC** | Serious injury, fatality, or incidents requiring immediate reporting |
| **Transport Canada** | Reportable aviation occurrences (per CARs) |
| **Energy Safety Canada** | Serious incidents affecting SECOR |
| **Clients** | Per contract requirements |
