## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes the process for developing, reviewing, and maintaining Formal Hazard Assessments (FHAs) for Aeria Solutions standard operations and activities.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to:
- All standard work activities requiring FHAs
- Development of new FHAs
- Annual review and update of existing FHAs
- FHA updates following incidents or changes

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 When to Develop an FHA

Develop or update an FHA when:
- Starting a new work activity not covered by existing FHAs
- Introducing new equipment or processes
- Significant change to existing activity
- Incident reveals new hazards
- During annual review cycle

### 4.2 FHA Development Process

#### Step 1: Define the Scope
- Identify the activity or environment to be assessed
- Define boundaries (what's included, what's separate)
- Identify workers who perform the activity
- Gather relevant information (regulations, procedures, incident history)

#### Step 2: Assemble the Team
FHA development should include:
- Operations Manager (leads process)
- Workers who perform the activity
- Technical experts if needed
- Safety resource if available

#### Step 3: Identify Hazards
Use multiple methods:

| Method | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| **Task Analysis** | Break activity into steps; identify hazards at each step |
| **Physical Inspection** | Examine equipment, work area, materials |
| **Worker Input** | Interview experienced workers |
| **Incident Review** | Review past incidents, near-misses |
| **Standards Review** | Check regulations, manufacturer guidance |

Document all identified hazards.

#### Step 4: Assess Risk

For each hazard, assess:

**Likelihood:**
| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| High | Expected to occur; has happened |
| Medium | Could occur; has occurred in industry |
| Low | Unlikely but possible |

**Severity:**
| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| High | Fatality, permanent disability, major damage |
| Medium | Medical treatment, lost time, significant damage |
| Low | First aid, minor injury, minor damage |

**Risk Level:**
|  | Low Severity | Medium Severity | High Severity |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **High Likelihood** | Medium | High | Critical |
| **Medium Likelihood** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Low Likelihood** | Low | Low | Medium |

#### Step 5: Determine Controls

For each hazard, identify controls using the hierarchy:
1. Elimination
2. Substitution
3. Engineering
4. Administrative
5. PPE

Document:
- Control measure(s)
- Who is responsible
- When control applies
- How effectiveness is verified

#### Step 6: Assess Residual Risk

After controls applied, re-assess risk level.

| Residual Risk | Acceptability |
|:--------------|:--------------|
| Critical | Not acceptable; additional controls required |
| High | May proceed with supervisor approval and enhanced monitoring |
| Medium | Acceptable with documented controls |
| Low | Acceptable |

#### Step 7: Document Training Requirements

Identify training needed to:
- Understand hazards
- Apply controls
- Use equipment safely
- Respond to emergencies

#### Step 8: Document and Approve

Complete FHA document including:
- Activity description
- Hazards identified
- Risk assessments
- Controls
- Residual risk
- Training requirements
- Related procedures and PPE
- Approval signatures

### 4.3 FHA Review

FHAs shall be reviewed:

| Trigger | Review Type |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Annual** | Full review; update date and version |
| **Post-Incident** | Review relevant sections; update if needed |
| **Change** | Review affected sections; update as required |
| **Worker Request** | Review specific concerns |

### 4.4 FHA Numbering

FHAs are numbered by category:

| Series | Category |
|:-------|:---------|
| FHA-1.x | Work Environment & Personnel |
| FHA-2.x | Standard RPAS Operations |
| FHA-3.x | Complex/Specialized RPAS Operations |
| FHA-4.x | Equipment & Emergency |
| FHA-5.x | Survey & Data Collection |
| FHA-6.x | Specialized Field Operations |
| FHA-7.x | Industrial Site Operations |

### 4.5 Communication

- New and updated FHAs shall be communicated to affected workers
- FHAs shall be available for reference during work
- PICs shall review applicable FHAs during mission planning
- Workers shall acknowledge understanding during training
