## 1. Scope

This FHA covers the transport and storage of RPAS equipment including aircraft, batteries, ground control stations, payloads, and support equipment. Activities include vehicle loading/unloading, ground transport, air transport (commercial and charter), dangerous goods handling (lithium batteries), secure storage, and equipment staging at field locations.

## 4. Control Summary

### Critical Controls

| Control | Purpose | Responsible |
|:--------|:--------|:------------|
| TDG compliance | Legal battery transport | Operations Manager |
| LiPo-safe storage | Fire prevention | All personnel |
| Load securement | Prevent shifting cargo | Driver/loader |
| Pre-transport inspection | Verify equipment condition | PIC |
| Climate control | Prevent temperature damage | Facility Manager |
| Two-person lift policy | Prevent manual handling injury | All personnel |
| Inventory management | Track equipment condition and location | Operations Manager |

### PPE Requirements

| PPE | When Required |
|:----|:--------------|
| Steel-toe boots | All loading/unloading activities |
| Work gloves | Handling cases and equipment |
| High-visibility vest | Near vehicles, parking areas |
| Safety glasses | When securing loads |
| Back support belt | Heavy lifting (optional) |
