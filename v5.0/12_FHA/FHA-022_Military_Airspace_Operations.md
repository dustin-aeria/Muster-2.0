## 1. Scope

This Formal Hazard Assessment addresses the risks associated with conducting Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (RPAS) operations near National Defence Aerodromes, within or adjacent to military airspace, and in areas where military flight training or operations occur. This assessment covers operations within Class F restricted and advisory airspace, coordination with military Air Traffic Control (ATC), compliance with NOTAMs affecting military operations, and communication protocols required when operating near military facilities.

This FHA applies to all Aeria Solutions Ltd personnel involved in planning, conducting, and supporting RPAS operations that may interact with or be affected by military aviation activities.

## 2. Risk Matrix

**Likelihood (L)**

| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| 1 | Rare — May occur only in exceptional circumstances |
| 2 | Unlikely — Could occur but not expected |
| 3 | Possible — Might occur at some time |
| 4 | Likely — Will probably occur in most circumstances |
| 5 | Almost Certain — Expected to occur in most circumstances |

**Severity (S)**

| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| 1 | Negligible — No injury, minimal operational impact |
| 2 | Minor — First aid injury, minor operational delays |
| 3 | Moderate — Medical treatment, significant operational impact |
| 4 | Major — Serious injury, major operational disruption |
| 5 | Catastrophic — Fatality, complete operational failure, national security breach |

**Risk Level = Likelihood x Severity**

| Risk Score | Risk Level | Action Required |
|:-----------|:-----------|:----------------|
| 1-4 | Low | Monitor and manage through routine procedures |
| 5-9 | Medium | Specific controls required, management oversight |
| 10-16 | High | Senior management attention, significant controls |
| 17-25 | Extreme | Immediate action required, operation may not proceed |

## 3. Hazard Analysis

### 3.1 Pre-Flight Hazards

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Consequence | L | S | Risk | Controls | Residual Risk |
|:---|:-------|:------|:------------|:--|:--|:-----|:---------|:--------------|
| MIL-01 | Unauthorized Entry into Class F Restricted Airspace | Failure to identify restricted airspace boundaries during planning | Interference with military operations, security incident, prosecution | 3 | 5 | 15 High | Review Designated Airspace Handbook; plot all Class F airspace on flight planning map; obtain authorization prior to entry; verify boundaries with NAV CANADA | 4 Medium |
| MIL-02 | Missed Military NOTAM Information | Incomplete NOTAM briefing, failure to check military-specific NOTAMs | Unknowing entry into active military exercise area, collision risk | 3 | 5 | 15 High | Comprehensive NOTAM review including CYHQ and military-specific publications; verify active times of military airspace; document NOTAM review in flight log | 4 Medium |
| MIL-03 | Inadequate Coordination with Military ATC | No prior contact with controlling military authority, unclear authorization process | Denied access, operational delays, unauthorized operations | 4 | 3 | 12 High | Contact Terminal Control Area (TCA) or military aerodrome control minimum 48 hours prior; obtain written authorization; confirm radio frequencies and contact procedures | 6 Medium |
| MIL-04 | Incorrect Radio Frequency Selection | Multiple frequencies for military facilities, confusion between active frequencies | Loss of communications, missed traffic advisories, airspace violation | 3 | 4 | 12 High | Pre-program all required military frequencies; verify active frequencies through ATIS or ATC contact; carry backup communication device | 6 Medium |
| MIL-05 | Failure to Identify Military Traffic Patterns | Unfamiliarity with military aerodrome procedures, non-standard patterns | Position conflict with military aircraft, collision risk | 3 | 5 | 15 High | Review Canada Flight Supplement for military aerodrome procedures; study published traffic patterns; brief crew on expected military traffic flow | 4 Medium |

## 4. Control Summary

### 4.1 Critical Controls

| Control Category | Required Controls |
|:-----------------|:------------------|
| Airspace Authorization | Written authorization from controlling military authority minimum 48 hours prior to operations |
| NOTAM Review | Comprehensive review of all NOTAMs including military-specific publications (CYHQ) |
| Communication Equipment | Primary and backup radio systems capable of military frequencies |
| Visual Observers | Trained observers briefed on military aircraft identification and traffic patterns |
| Flight Planning | Detailed flight plan filed with military ATC including emergency procedures |
| GPS Tracking | Continuous track logging for post-flight boundary verification |
| Lost Link Procedures | Pre-programmed return-to-home and emergency landing procedures |

### 4.2 Personal Protective Equipment

| PPE Item | Requirement | Application |
|:---------|:------------|:------------|
| High-Visibility Vest | Mandatory | All personnel on military aerodromes |
| Hearing Protection | Mandatory | Operations near active runways or helicopter operations |
| Safety Footwear | Mandatory | All personnel on military aerodromes |
| Communication Headset | Recommended | For continuous ATC monitoring |
