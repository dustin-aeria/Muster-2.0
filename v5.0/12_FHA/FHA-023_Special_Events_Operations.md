## 1. Scope

This Formal Hazard Assessment addresses the risks associated with conducting Remotely Piloted Aircraft System (RPAS) operations at or near advertised special events. Special events include but are not limited to air shows, concerts, festivals, sporting events, parades, public gatherings, fireworks displays, and any event that may result in the assembly of large numbers of people.

This assessment covers hazards related to crowd safety, temporary airspace restrictions (TFRs/NOTAMs), media and public interaction, security coordination, and the unique operational environment present at special events. This FHA applies to all Aeria Solutions Ltd personnel involved in planning, conducting, and supporting RPAS operations at special events.

## 2. Risk Matrix

**Likelihood (L)**

| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| 1 | Rare — May occur only in exceptional circumstances |
| 2 | Unlikely — Could occur but not expected |
| 3 | Possible — Might occur at some time |
| 4 | Likely — Will probably occur in most circumstances |
| 5 | Almost Certain — Expected to occur in most circumstances |

**Severity (S)**

| Rating | Description |
|:-------|:------------|
| 1 | Negligible — No injury, minimal operational impact |
| 2 | Minor — First aid injury, minor operational delays |
| 3 | Moderate — Medical treatment, significant operational impact |
| 4 | Major — Serious injury, major operational disruption, public incident |
| 5 | Catastrophic — Fatality, mass casualty potential, major public incident |

**Risk Level = Likelihood x Severity**

| Risk Score | Risk Level | Action Required |
|:-----------|:-----------|:----------------|
| 1-4 | Low | Monitor and manage through routine procedures |
| 5-9 | Medium | Specific controls required, management oversight |
| 10-16 | High | Senior management attention, significant controls |
| 17-25 | Extreme | Immediate action required, operation may not proceed |

## 3. Hazard Analysis

### 3.1 Pre-Flight Hazards

|:---|:-------|:------|:------------|:--|:--|:-----|:---------|:--------------|

| SE-02 | Inadequate Event Coordination | Failure to coordinate with event organizers, security, and emergency services | Operational conflicts, safety gaps, event disruption | 3 | 4 | 12 High | Attend pre-event coordination meetings; establish communications with event command; document coordination in writing; identify key contacts | 6 Medium |
| SE-03 | Missed Temporary Flight Restrictions | Failure to identify event-related NOTAMs or temporary airspace restrictions | Airspace violation, interference with other aircraft, enforcement action | 3 | 5 | 15 High | Comprehensive NOTAM check for event dates; verify with NAV CANADA; document airspace status; confirm authorization for operation within TFR | 4 Medium |

### 3.2 In-Flight Hazards — Crowd Safety

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Consequence | L | S | Risk | Controls | Residual Risk |
|:---|:-------|:------|:------------|:--|:--|:-----|:---------|:--------------|
| SE-06 | RPAS Crash into Crowd | Aircraft malfunction, loss of control, pilot error | Multiple injuries, fatalities, major public incident | 2 | 5 | 10 High | Maintain minimum 30m horizontal distance from crowds; use aircraft with proven reliability; conduct pre-flight airworthiness check; avoid overflying crowds unless specifically authorized | 4 Medium |
| SE-07 | Battery Failure over Assembly Area | Battery defect, improper charging, cold weather | Uncontrolled descent into crowd | 2 | 5 | 10 High | Use batteries at <80% capacity cycles; pre-flight battery inspection; limit flight time to 70% of rated capacity; plan flight path with emergency landing zones away from crowds | 4 Medium |
| SE-08 | Loss of Control Link over Crowds | RF interference from event electronics, distance | Flyaway or uncontrolled descent | 3 | 5 | 15 High | Site RF survey prior to event; use frequency-hopping spread spectrum; set failsafe to return-to-home away from crowds; maintain line-of-sight | 6 Medium |
| SE-09 | Crowd Movement into Operations Area | Inadequate barriers, public curiosity, crowd surge | Public entering hazard zone, potential collision | 4 | 4 | 16 High | Establish physical barriers minimum 10m radius; deploy visual observers at perimeter; coordinate with event security for crowd control; use signage and PA announcements | 8 Medium |
| SE-10 | Public Distraction by RPAS | Spectators distracted, looking up instead of at environment | Trip hazards, crowd management issues, complaints | 4 | 2 | 8 Medium | Coordinate flights with event programming; brief event staff; avoid prolonged hover over crowd sightlines; maintain discrete operations where possible | 4 Medium |

## 4. Control Summary

### 4.1 Critical Controls

| Control Category | Required Controls |
|:-----------------|:------------------|
| Authorization | SFOC with specific event authorization; written approval from event organizer |
| Airspace Coordination | Coordination with NAV CANADA, event air boss, and aviation coordinator |
| Crowd Separation | Minimum 30m horizontal distance from assembled persons unless specifically authorized |
| Physical Barriers | Secured perimeter minimum 10m radius around launch/recovery area |
| Emergency Coordination | Pre-coordination with event medical, fire, and security services |
| Communication | Direct communication with event command and aviation coordinator |
| Weather Monitoring | Continuous monitoring with conservative abort criteria |

### 4.2 Personal Protective Equipment

| PPE Item | Requirement | Application |
|:---------|:------------|:------------|
| High-Visibility Vest | Mandatory | All flight crew and support personnel |
| Safety Footwear | Mandatory | All personnel in event area |
| Hearing Protection | As required | High-noise environments |
| Sun Protection | Recommended | Outdoor events |
| Communication Headset | Mandatory | For continuous event coordination |
| Credential/ID Badge | Mandatory | All personnel at event |
