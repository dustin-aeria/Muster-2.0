## 1. Scope

This FHA covers manual handling activities associated with RPAS operations including lifting, carrying, pushing, pulling, and positioning of heavy equipment such as Pelican cases, aircraft systems, generators, ground control stations, payload equipment, tripods, and support gear. Activities include equipment setup, vehicle loading/unloading, field deployment, and equipment staging.

## 4. Control Summary

### Critical Controls

| Control | Purpose | Responsible |
|:--------|:--------|:------------|
| Two-person lift policy (>18kg) | Prevent individual overload | All personnel |
| Mechanical lifting aids | Reduce manual handling | Operations Manager |
| Lifting technique training | Proper body mechanics | All personnel |
| Pre-lift assessment | Identify hazards before lifting | All personnel |
| Route clearance | Safe path for carrying | Person performing lift |
| Weight labeling on cases | Know load before lifting | Operations Manager |
| Team lift communication protocol | Coordinated lifting | Team leads |

### PPE Requirements

| PPE | When Required |
|:----|:--------------|
| Steel-toe boots | All manual handling |
| Work gloves | Handling equipment with pinch points |
| High-visibility vest | Near vehicles/traffic |
| Back support belt | Heavy repetitive lifting (optional) |
| Safety glasses | When securing/releasing latches |
